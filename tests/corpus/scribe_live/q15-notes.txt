The Riverbend Community Tool Library

Borrowing a tool

Any resident of Riverbend can borrow up to three tools at a time. Bring a library card and a recent letter with your address on it the first time you visit.

Most tools go out for seven days. Ladders and the tile saw go out for three, because the waiting list for them is always long.

Returning a tool

Clean the tool before you bring it back, and tell the volunteer at the desk if anything is broken or missing. Nobody is charged for honest wear.

Opening hours

The library opens on Tuesday and Thursday evenings and all day on Saturday. It closes for the first week of January for the annual count.
