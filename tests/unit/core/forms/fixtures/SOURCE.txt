Where these fixtures come from
==============================

All of them come from QUILL Accessible Forms (QAF), S:\code\baf on Jeff's
machine, which was named BITS Accessible Forms (BAF) until November 2026; the
older examples were copied before the rename, so they and their notes below use
BAF's names. QAF is Copyright
(c) 2024-2026 Jeff Bishop, released under the MIT License (relicensed from
GPL-2.0-or-later in November 2026), the same licence as QUILL. They are
copied here for testing. The choice-library data in
quill/core/forms/libraries/qaf_libraries.json and the schema in
quill/core/forms/schema/form.schema.json have the same origin.

conformance/
  A subset of QAF's form model conformance suite, copied unchanged from
  tests/conformance on QAF's chore/rename-qaf branch (commit beb3e2c,
  2026-11-05), in its renamed layout (qaf/, .qaf files, the qaf-* kinds and
  the "qaf" case key): both expression files, two valid models, the
  invalid-model and behaviour cases, four canonical and four legacy pairs, and
  the error cases. The schema in quill/core/forms/schema/form.schema.json was
  refreshed from the same commit. manifest.json here lists only those, in
  QAF's manifest shape. test_forms_conformance.py runs it on every machine,
  and runs QAF's whole manifest too when QAF is on the machine (falling back
  to feat/json-form-model's older bfl/ layout).

NAME.bfl, NAME.baf.json, NAME.model.json
  Twenty BFL examples copied unchanged from BAF's main branch (commit
  461375e): the fenced blocks of docs/user-guide/bfl-language-guide.md and
  the $bfl literals of tests/unit/BFL/ParserTest.php.
  - NAME.baf.json: what BAF's OLDER parser and formatter (includes/bfl, still
    used by Structure Mode) make of it, produced by baf_php_harness.php
    running BAF's own PHP read-only.
  - NAME.model.json: the form model BFL 1 reads from it (BAF's new reader,
    ported in quill/core/forms/bfl_read.py).
