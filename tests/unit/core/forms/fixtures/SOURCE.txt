Where these fixtures come from
==============================

All of them come from BITS Accessible Forms (BAF), S:\code\baf on Jeff's
machine. BAF is copyright BITS (Blind Information Technology Solutions) and
Community Access, released under GPL-2.0-or-later. They are copied here for
testing; QUILL itself is MIT-licensed, so reusing them here rests on the same
owners' permission. The choice-library data in
quill/core/forms/libraries/baf_libraries.json and the schema in
quill/core/forms/schema/form.schema.json have the same origin.

conformance/
  A subset of BAF's form model conformance suite, copied unchanged from
  tests/conformance on BAF's feat/json-form-model branch (commit 8ec970d,
  2026-11-04): the expression cases, two valid models, the invalid-model and
  behaviour cases, three canonical and three legacy BFL pairs, and the BFL
  error cases. manifest.json here lists only those, in BAF's manifest shape.
  test_forms_conformance.py runs it on every machine, and runs BAF's whole
  manifest too when BAF is on the machine.

NAME.bfl, NAME.baf.json, NAME.model.json
  Twenty BFL examples copied unchanged from BAF's main branch (commit
  461375e): the fenced blocks of docs/user-guide/bfl-language-guide.md and
  the $bfl literals of tests/unit/BFL/ParserTest.php.
  - NAME.baf.json: what BAF's OLDER parser and formatter (includes/bfl, still
    used by Structure Mode) make of it, produced by baf_php_harness.php
    running BAF's own PHP read-only.
  - NAME.model.json: the form model BFL 1 reads from it (BAF's new reader,
    ported in quill/core/forms/bfl_read.py).
