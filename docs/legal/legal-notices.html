QUILL for All

QUILL for All is an independent open-source project by Community Access. It is an accessibility-focused editor that helps people write, edit, convert, compare, and publish documents in a screen-reader-friendly environment, and it is the editor at the heart of the QuillVille family of apps.

QUILL for All and every app in the family are free and never sold. Donations only cover the cost of the free AI service.

Third-party components

The family includes software, data, models and sounds from other projects, each under its own licence. They are listed, with their licences, copyright holders and what each licence asks of us, in the Third-Party Notices.

The same notices reach people in three places:

  • In every app: Help, then About, then the Licences and Notices button. In QUILL itself, Help > Open Third-Party Notices (also a button in Help > About QUILL) opens them in a new document tab.
  • Beside the program: every installer and portable copy puts THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and LICENSE.txt in the app’s own folder.
  • In the repository: this folder. The notices are generated from quill/data/third_party/components.json by quill/tools/build_third_party_notices.py, and a test fails if any copy drifts from that catalogue or if a build ships a library it does not list.
