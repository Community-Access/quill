# Legal Documents

The legal documents for QUILL for All and every app in the QuillVille family.
Each one exists once, as Markdown, with an HTML and EPUB copy beside it.

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Licence and Legal Notices](legal-notices.md) | The family's MIT licence and copyright holder, the independence notice, and where the licences reach people in every app. |
| [Trademarks and Naming](TRADEMARKS.md) | How to use the QUILL family's names, the product list, and how the family refers to other people's marks. |
| [Privacy Statement](PRIVACY.md) | What the apps keep on your computer, every network request they make without being asked, and what the AI, dictation and YouTube features send. |
| [Responsible and Ethical AI Use](RESPONSIBLE_AI_USE.md) | The rules for using the AI features and the controls that keep you in charge. |
| [Third-Party Notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) | Every third-party component in the family, its licence, copyright holder and obligations, with the full licence texts. Generated; do not edit. |

Generated plain-text copies of the third-party notices also live here:

- THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt -- the family document as plain text.
- THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-QUILL.txt -- the file QUILL's installer and portable zip
  ship beside quill.exe. Each app's own copy is standalone/APP/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

Planning and working notes do not belong here: they live in docs/planning
(the TINDRA naming plan moved there on 2026-10-26).
