Chapter 19: Scribe for Documents

Some documents are hard to read with a screen reader. A scanned letter is only a picture of text. A badly made PDF reads its columns in the wrong order, and its tables come out as a jumble of words. QUILL can fix a lot of this on your own computer. When it cannot, Scribe for Documents can.

Scribe for Documents is a service from Pneuma Solutions. You send it a document, and it sends back an accessible one: Word, accessible HTML, tagged PDF, EPUB, Kindle, DAISY with audio, MP3 audio or braille. It rebuilds the headings, the reading order and the tables, and it can describe the pictures. QUILL’s Scribe commands are Powered by Scribe for Documents.

Nothing leaves your computer until you say so, every time. Before each upload QUILL shows you what will be sent, and where, and waits for you to press Send and Convert.

This chapter covers everything: your Scribe account, every command and window, what you hear at each stage, six walkthroughs, the keys, privacy, and what each problem means. QUILL Lite has most of it too; its own guide has a full chapter on Scribe in QUILL Lite.

What Scribe for Documents is

Scribe for Documents reads a document, works out its structure, and writes it out again in the format you ask for. It runs on Pneuma Solutions’ computers, not yours. So it can do work that is too heavy for a laptop: it reads scanned pages, rebuilds tables, writes picture descriptions with AI, and records an audio book with a natural voice.

You use your own Scribe account and pay Scribe for your own pages. QUILL never takes payment, and it never sees your Scribe password. What Scribe makes is yours. QUILL saves it in your own folders.

When to use Scribe, and when QUILL is enough

QUILL already converts a great deal for free, on your own computer, with nothing uploaded. Try that first.

You have Try first, free and local Use Scribe when
A Word file, an EPUB or a PDF with real text File > Open, or File > Import > Import / Convert Document (OCR)… The result has its headings, tables or reading order wrong
A scanned PDF or a photo of a page The free OCR QUILL offers, on your computer The OCR text is a mess, or you need the tables and headings back
A document you need as DAISY with audio, MP3, tagged PDF or Kindle QUILL makes EPUB, Word, braille and text DAISY itself You want a format QUILL cannot make
A document for braille QUILL’s own braille translation You want Scribe’s braille tables and page layout

Scribe costs pages, and the document goes to the United States. QUILL’s own tools cost nothing and keep everything on your computer. That is why QUILL always offers the free way first.

Signing in to Scribe

You sign in on Scribe’s own page, in your own web browser. QUILL never shows that page inside itself, and it never sees what you type there.

  1. Choose Tools > Scribe for Documents > Sign In…. You do not have to do this first: any Scribe command that needs you signed in starts the sign-in for you.
  2. Your browser opens at Scribe for Documents, and QUILL says “Your browser is open at Scribe for Documents. Sign in there, then come back to QUILL.”
  3. Sign in, or make a new account, in the browser. The browser tab then says “Signed in. You can close this tab and return to QUILL.”
  4. Come back to QUILL. The small window now says, for example, “Signed in to Scribe for Documents as Sam Lee. 100 pages left.” Focus is on Continue.
  5. Press Continue. If a command started the sign-in, it carries on.

The sign-in window has four buttons, though you see only the ones that make sense at the time:

  • Copy Sign-in Link copies Scribe’s sign-in address. Use it when the browser did not open, or when you want to sign in with a different browser. Paste it there.
  • Try Again starts again, after the sign-in timed out (“Sign-in timed out. Try again?”) or failed.
  • Continue closes the window once you are signed in.
  • Cancel stops waiting. You are not signed in.

One sign-in covers every QUILL app on this computer, QUILL Lite included. It is kept in Windows’ secure store, never in a settings file.

Sign Out (Tools > Scribe for Documents > Sign Out) forgets the sign-in for every QUILL app on this computer. If conversions are still waiting, QUILL asks first, for example: “Two conversions are waiting. Signing out stops QUILL waiting for them; they still finish in your Scribe account.” Answer Yes to sign out anyway.

If your organization pays

If you belong to an organization on Scribe, such as a college or an employer, your pages may come from the organization’s shared credits. QUILL says so beside the count, for example “1,240 pages left, using credits from Northside College.” In the Convert dialog the line reads “This uses about 34 of the 1,240 pages left in Northside College’s credits.”

Your organization also sets how long your documents are kept, from 1 to 30 days.

What you can send

Scribe takes 23 kinds of file:

Kind File types
PDF PDF, password-protected ones too
Pictures JPEG, GIF, PNG, TIFF, BMP, DCX, PCX, JPEG 2000 and DjVu
Text documents DOC, DOCX, RTF, ODT and TXT
Spreadsheets XLS, XLSX and ODS
Presentations PPT, PPTX and ODP
Ebooks EPUB and MOBI

A few rules:

  • No web pages. Scribe does not take HTML, as a file or as a link. QUILL says so before anything is sent: “page.html: Scribe for Documents does not take web pages. Save it as Word or PDF first.”
  • Up to 500 MB a file. QUILL checks the size first, so a file that is too big is never sent.
  • A link must point straight at a file, such as an address ending in .pdf. A page behind a sign-in will not work.
  • There is no page limit except the pages you have.

The file picker in Convert a File shows only the files Scribe takes. Its Files of type list can narrow it to PDF, Pictures, Text documents, Spreadsheets, Presentations or Ebooks.

What you can get back

Each format has a check box in the Convert dialog. You can choose several at once.

DAISY is DAISY 3, with text and audio together, in a zip file. When you send it to Quill Media Player, QUILL unpacks the zip into a folder beside it and opens the book there.

Results are saved in Documents\Scribe for Documents, in a folder named after the document. You can choose another folder in Options. QUILL never writes over a file that is already there: it adds “(2)” to the new name.

Each result is named after your file, not after the title Scribe reads from inside it. So Notes.pdf gives Notes.docx and Notes.brf, even when Scribe calls the document something else; a scanned page’s title can start with a stamp or a handwritten note. The offline web page is named Notes-offline.html, or Notes-offline.zip when Scribe sends it as a zip.

Pictures in the accessible HTML. Scribe’s accessible HTML points at its pictures by name, but the pictures are not sent with it. The Web page for offline reading is the same document with its pictures inside. When you ask for both, QUILL opens the accessible HTML, and Next Steps opens the offline web page in your browser, pictures and all; the result sentence ends: “Its pictures are in the Web page for offline reading; Next Steps opens that in your browser.” When you ask for the accessible HTML alone, the sentence ends: “The accessible HTML came without its pictures; Download Formats in My Scribe Documents can add the Web page for offline reading, which has them.”

The Web page for offline reading never opens in QUILL as text: every picture is inside it, so each would be a single very long line. When it is the only thing there is to open, Open Result, and Open in My Scribe Documents, open it in your browser: “Opening the Web page for offline reading in your browser.” Open in My Scribe Documents opens what Open Result would, Word before the Kindle book, not just the file downloaded last.

A Kindle (MOBI) book is for older Kindles. The result sentence says so: “A Kindle (MOBI) book is for older Kindles: copy it to the Kindle’s documents folder over USB. Amazon’s Send to Kindle takes the EPUB instead.” QUILL opens it too, as a document you can read here: see Kindle books in Opening, saving and converting files. When nothing that was made opens by itself, the sentence ends “Open Folder in Scribe Conversions shows it.”

The braille Scribe makes is American English Braille (EBAE) grade 2, not UEB, unless you choose another table. If a BRF has characters that are not braille cells, such as a tab or an accented letter copied from the document, QUILL keeps them and says how many when the file opens: “4 characters are not braille cells; Validate BRF Layout lists them.” Braille > Validate BRF Layout… goes to each one. A 32-cell BRF opens as 32 cells a line.

Options decides whether results open by themselves. See Scribe for Documents Options.

The Convert dialog, control by control

Every way of converting opens one dialog, Convert with Scribe for Documents. It is the same in QUILL and QUILL Lite. Here it is in Tab order.

What will be sent

The first control is a read-only text box, and focus starts there. Read it line by line with the arrow keys. It says exactly what is about to happen, for example:

“Notes.pdf (2.1 MB, 34 pages) will be sent to Scribe for Documents (Pneuma Solutions) and converted in the United States. It stays in your Scribe account under your retention setting (30 days unless you changed it) until you delete it. Pictures may be described by AI. Pneuma does not use documents to train AI.”

It can also say:

  • For several files, how many and which, and that they go one after another without asking again.
  • A caution when a file’s name suggests private, medical, legal or financial papers, such as “tax-return.pdf”.
  • Which of the files you chose cannot be sent, and why, after “Not sent:”.
  • Before your very first upload, the privacy notes from Privacy, retention and deleting.
  • For a document already in your account: “No file is sent. Scribe makes these formats from Notes, already in your account.”

It always ends with the copyright line: “Convert only documents you own, have permission for, or that are out of copyright.”

Pressing Enter in this box is the same as pressing Send and Convert.

What you want back

A group of nine check boxes, one for each format in What you can get back. A command made for one format starts with it checked: Word for Import, Tagged PDF for Export, and Braille (BRF) for Translate. The other commands start with the formats you chose last time, or your Default formats the very first time.

For a document already in your account, a format you have already made says “, already made”, and the others say “, no pages used”.

Read while it converts

Read while it converts, in a new tab (adds Accessible HTML) opens a read-only tab that fills with the text while Scribe works. Checking it checks Accessible HTML too. It is there only when you convert one file, not for a link or several files. See Reading while it converts.

Settings shown

A read-only line that says which groups of settings follow, for example “Settings below: Language and voice, Braille”. It changes as you check formats, without saying anything.

The settings start as your Scribe account’s own defaults, the ones on the Scribe website. Anything you changed last time in QUILL is put on top. Hidden groups keep their values.

The Print setting

Shown when Word or Tagged PDF is checked. Large print makes the Word and tagged PDF in large print.

The Pictures and maths settings

Always shown.

  • Describe the pictures: Scribe’s AI writes a description of each picture. If you have turned AI off in QUILL, this starts unchecked and says why. You may still check it, because it is Scribe’s own work, not QUILL’s AI.
  • Mathematics (beta): Scribe’s beta setting for mathematics. Today it does not make MathML in any format: an equation comes through as a picture whose alt text reads the equation in words, such as “w subscript 0 = [n, n - 1, …, 2, 1]”, and maths inside a sentence is plain text. That is true with the setting on or off.
  • Email me when it is finished (Scribe’s own email): Scribe sends you its own email when the work is done, one email for each format. It starts unchecked, even when your Scribe account’s default is to email you, unless you checked it last time: QUILL already tells you when each result is ready.

Language, picture descriptions, maths and large print shape the document from the start. So they are fixed when it is uploaded. When you make another format of a document already in your account, those four are hidden and a read-only line, Fixed when Notes was uploaded, lists their values instead. The other settings apply to formats not made yet. Formats already made keep their own settings.

Account defaults

Also save as my Scribe account defaults (changes your Scribe website settings too) is off every time the dialog opens. Leave it off, and your settings are used for this conversion and remembered in QUILL. Check it, and Scribe’s own defaults change too.

Password

Shown only when a PDF needs a password. When QUILL can tell, it asks the moment you press Send and Convert: “Notes.pdf needs its password. Type it, then press Send and Convert.” The field is labelled Password for Notes.pdf.

Show password shows what you typed as plain text, so you can check it with braille or typing echo. The password is used for this one upload. It is never saved, and never written to a log.

Pages and Buy Pages

A read-only line, for example “This uses about 34 of your 100 pages.” It updates as you check formats, without saying anything. For a file whose page count QUILL cannot read, it says only how many pages you have. For another format of a document already in your account it says “No pages used: this document is already in your Scribe account.”

Buy Pages opens Scribe’s purchase page in your browser. When you come back to the dialog, the count is read again.

Send and Convert

Send and Convert sends the file and starts the work. Pressing it is your consent. For a document already in your account the button says Convert, because nothing is uploaded. Cancel closes the dialog, and nothing is sent.

If no format is checked, QUILL says “Choose at least one format to get back.” and puts you on the Word check box.

The Scribe for Documents commands

Convert a File

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Convert a File… opens a file picker that shows only the files Scribe takes. You can choose several at once. Then the Convert dialog opens.

Convert This Document

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Convert This Document… sends the document you are working on. If it has changes, or was never saved, QUILL saves it first and asks for a name if it needs one. A Word, PDF or other file that Scribe takes is sent as it is. A Markdown or HTML document is sent as Word, made with Pandoc, or as plain text when Pandoc is not installed.

Convert from a Link

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Convert from a Link… asks for “The address of a file (a PDF or a document, not a web page or a page behind a sign-in)”. Type or paste an address that starts with https:// or http://, and press OK. Scribe fetches the file itself. Anything else is refused with “That is not a web address. Nothing was sent.”

Convert with Scribe from Import

File > Import > Convert with Scribe for Documents… is the Scribe step of Import. It converts one file, and the result opens as a new, unsaved tab named after the file, such as “Notes (Scribe)”. The result sentence ends “Word is open in a new tab.”, and the status bar says, for example, “Opened Notes.pdf from Scribe for Documents, which rebuilt it; with your consent it was uploaded and stays in your Scribe account.”

Export with Scribe

File > Export > With Scribe for Documents… sends the document you are working on, saved first, as in Convert This Document. It is how QUILL gives you tagged PDF, Kindle, DAISY with audio and large print, which QUILL cannot make itself. QUILL’s own exports of EPUB, Word, braille and text-only DAISY are still in the Export menu, and they stay on your computer.

Translate with Scribe

Tools > Braille > Translate with Scribe for Documents… sends the document you are working on for braille, with Scribe’s tables and page settings. The braille opens in QUILL’s braille view, where the proofing and checking tools work on it like any braille file.

Scribe Status and Repeat Last Scribe Message

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Scribe Status says what the conversion running now is doing, once, without opening anything: “Notes.pdf, adding image descriptions, 40 percent, about 2 minutes.” With nothing running it says “No Scribe for Documents conversions are running.”

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Repeat Last Scribe Message says the last thing Scribe for Documents said again. Before anything has been said, you hear “Nothing from Scribe for Documents yet.”

Both are good commands to give a key of your own in the Keymap Editor.

Open My Scribe Account in Browser

Tools > Scribe for Documents > Open My Scribe Account in Browser opens your account on the Scribe website, already signed in. Go there to change how long documents are kept, buy pages, change your account defaults, or delete your account.

About Scribe for Documents

Tools > Scribe for Documents > About Scribe for Documents shows what Scribe is, who makes it, and the privacy notes.

When a PDF looks scanned

When you open a PDF that has little or no readable text, QUILL offers to read it. The same happens when Import / Convert Document (OCR) finds no text. With Scribe available, the question has three choices:

  • Run free OCR on this computer (nothing is uploaded)
  • Convert with Scribe for Documents (rebuilds headings, tables and picture descriptions; uses about 12 pages of your Scribe pages)
  • Open the text there is

The free choice is first. The Scribe choice opens the Convert dialog, so you still see what will be sent before anything goes. To leave Scribe out of this question, uncheck Offer Scribe when a PDF has little or no text in Options.

Scribe Conversions

Window > Scribe Conversions, or Tools > Scribe for Documents > Conversions, opens a window that lists every Scribe job on this computer, from every QUILL app. It stays open beside your document. Jobs carry on when you close it, and when you close QUILL.

The window has:

  • Conversions, a list with three columns: File, Formats and Stage. Each row’s Stage changes only when the stage changes, and the rows never move under you. When there is nothing to list, a line says “No Scribe for Documents conversions.”
  • Details, a read-only box with everything about the job you are on: its stage, formats, percent, time left, Scribe’s latest message, the files made, the folder, and an error code if there was a problem.
  • A row of buttons for the job you are on, then Close.

In the list, Enter opens the result, Delete removes the row, and the Applications key or Shift+F10 opens a menu with every command. Escape closes the window and takes you back to where you were.

Several files, one after another

Choose several files in Convert a File, and they go in one Convert dialog, with one consent. They are sent one after another, each starting only when the one before has finished or failed. You are not asked again. Pneuma agreed to this way of working.

Each file’s start, result or problem is said as it happens. A file that fails does not stop the rest. When the last one is done you hear one sentence for them all, for example “Scribe for Documents finished 3 files. Made: Notes.pdf and Map.png. Not made: Old.doc; Scribe Conversions has the details.”

If Scribe asks QUILL to slow down, QUILL waits a minute and says so once: “Scribe for Documents asked QUILL to slow down; trying again in a minute.” The Details box shows the time it will try again.

Closing QUILL while a job runs

Closing QUILL does not lose a job. Scribe carries on, and the job stays on the list. The next time a QUILL app starts, it picks the job up and downloads what was made. Then it says, once, for example “Scribe for Documents finished 2 documents while QUILL was closed: Notes and Chapter 2, in Documents\Scribe for Documents.”

Next Steps

When a job has made something, Next Steps… in Scribe Conversions lists what you can do with it. Nothing runs until you choose it, and each one is a separate choice.

What was made Next Steps offers
Word, Accessible HTML or EPUB Read Aloud with QUILL’s Voices, Check Accessibility (GLOW Audit) and Summarize with Your AI…
Braille (BRF) Open in the Braille View
Kindle (MOBI) Open the Kindle Book (MOBI)
MP3 audio or DAISY Send to Media Player
Word or Accessible HTML, where Quill Beacon is installed Save a Beacon Place…

Each one opens the file in QUILL first, or goes to its tab if it is already open, and then runs the command. When a job made more than one of Word, HTML and EPUB, each step names its format, such as “Read Aloud with QUILL’s Voices (Word)”. Read Aloud always starts from the top of the document. Summarize uses the AI you chose in Choose Your AI, which asks first as it always does, and is not offered when AI is turned off. Scribe is not involved in that step. If the Media Player cannot be opened, QUILL says so and the file stays in its folder.

Save a Beacon Place

Quill Beacon keeps places you want to come back to. Save a Beacon Place… keeps one in a Scribe result: its start, or one of its headings. It is offered for Word and accessible HTML, when Quill Beacon is installed.

  1. In Scribe Conversions, choose the job and press Next Steps….
  2. Choose Save a Beacon Place…. When the job made both Word and HTML, each names its format, such as Save a Beacon Place (Word)….
  3. A list opens, Save a Beacon Place, labelled “Save the place at:”. The first row is “The start of the document”; then come the headings, their words first and their level after, such as “Methods, heading level 2”. A heading that appears twice says which it is: “Results, heading level 2, second”. A document with no headings skips the list.
  4. Arrow to the place and press Enter. You hear once: “Saved a Beacon place: Notes, at Methods.”

The place is in Quill Beacon, under Scribe Documents. It remembers the Scribe document, so if the file is ever gone Beacon offers to download it again from your Scribe account, with no pages used. A running Beacon shows the new place the next time it reads its list, without a word.

Another format, with no pages used

Because pages are charged once for each document, Download Formats… is the cheap way to get a new format. The Convert dialog opens with “No file is sent”, each format you already have says “, already made”, and the rest say “, no pages used”. Press Convert. The steps are in the walkthrough Another format of a document in your account.

Reading while it converts

Scribe makes the HTML first, and sends it to QUILL a piece at a time. Check Read while it converts, in a new tab in the Convert dialog, and you can start reading at once.

  • A new tab opens, named, for example, “Notes (Scribe, converting)”. It is read-only.
  • Text is added only a whole paragraph, heading or list item at a time, at the end. Your place does not move, the braille line does not change under you, and nothing new is spoken.
  • When the text is all there, the tab is named “Notes (Scribe, complete)”. If the conversion fails partway, it is named “Notes (Scribe, incomplete)” and keeps what arrived.
  • The result sentence says it: “Notes is complete in its tab; Replace with Finished Document, in Tools, swaps in the finished one”.

Replace with Finished Document

The streamed text is a quick first look. The finished Word or HTML can have more, such as a title and picture descriptions. Tools > Scribe for Documents > Replace with Finished Document swaps the finished document into the tab, and keeps your place at the same paragraph. You hear “Replaced with the finished document.” The tab is then named “Notes (Scribe)”, and you can edit and save it like any new document.

If the finished document is not ready, you hear “The finished document is not ready yet.” In any other tab, you hear that the command works in a tab that is reading a Scribe for Documents conversion.

The watch folder

A watch folder can send every document that lands in it to Scribe. This is the only standing consent Scribe has in QUILL, and you give it one profile at a time. Watch folders are part of QUILL’s feature profiles; if Tools > Watch Folder is missing, choose a fuller profile in Profiles and Features.

  1. Choose Tools > Watch Folder > Watch Folder Profiles… and press Add….
  2. Give the profile a name and choose its Watch folder.
  3. In Action, choose Convert with Scribe for Documents.
  4. In the group Scribe for Documents makes, check the formats you want.
  5. Read Scribe for Documents consent details. It says that every document that arrives is sent with nobody at the computer, and uses pages from your account.
  6. Check I agree to send this folder’s files to Scribe for Documents (Pneuma Solutions), and press OK.

Things to know:

  • One at a time. Files go through the same queue as everything else, one after another, and they show in Scribe Conversions.
  • Only what Scribe takes. Any other file is left alone for the profile’s other work.
  • What you hear is the watch folder’s usual result, such as “Scribe for Documents made Notes.docx in Documents\Scribe for Documents\Notes”. Nothing is said twice, and nothing opens by itself.
  • Sign in first. If you are not signed in, you hear once for the profile, not once a file: “Watch profile needs you to sign in to Scribe for Documents before it can send files. Choose Tools, Scribe for Documents, Sign In.”
  • No dialogs. A file that needs a password waits in Scribe Conversions.
  • Settings. The profile chooses the formats. The other settings, such as language and voice, are your Scribe account’s defaults, read when each file is sent, with any you set in Scribe for Documents Options on top.
  • Retries. If the watch folder tries a file again, QUILL reports the job Scribe already has rather than sending the file a second time.
  • Stopping. Uncheck the consent box, or disable the profile, and nothing more is sent.

Preview (dry run) in the profile says what would happen to a file without sending it.

From Explorer: Send To

QUILL’s installer can add Convert with Scribe for Documents to Explorer’s Send To menu. It is a choice on the installer’s page of extra options, unchecked to start with: check Add “Convert with Scribe for Documents” to Send To.

  1. In File Explorer, select one or more documents.
  2. Press the Applications key, choose Send to, then Convert with Scribe for Documents.
  3. QUILL starts, or the QUILL that is already running comes to the front, and the Convert dialog opens once for all the files you sent, on What will be sent. Files Scribe does not take are named there, after “Not sent:”.
  4. Choose your formats and press Send and Convert, as from Convert a File.

If QUILL has a dialog open when the files arrive, it does not open another on top of it. You hear once, for example, “Convert with Scribe for Documents is waiting until you close Find and Replace.”, and the Convert dialog opens when you close it.

To take the item off the Send To menu, run the installer again and uncheck it, or delete the shortcut from your Send To folder. QUILL Lite’s installer does not add it yet.

Each walkthrough says what to press and what you hear. Your file names and numbers will be different.

A scanned PDF to Word

  1. Choose File > Open and open the scanned PDF, here “Letter.pdf”.
  2. QUILL finds little text and asks. Choose Convert with Scribe for Documents (rebuilds headings, tables and picture descriptions; uses about 3 pages of your Scribe pages), and press OK.

A textbook to DAISY audio for the Media Player

A PDF to braille, proofed in the braille view

A password-protected PDF

  1. Choose Tools > Scribe for Documents > Convert a File… and choose the PDF, here “Statement.pdf”.
  2. Choose your formats and press Send and Convert.
  3. QUILL sees the PDF is locked and says “Statement.pdf needs its password. Type it, then press Send and Convert.” You are in Password for Statement.pdf.
  4. Type the password. To read back what you typed, check Show password and read it with braille or the arrow keys.

Privacy, retention and deleting

This is what QUILL tells you before your first upload, and what is in About in Options.

  • Where it goes. Pneuma Solutions processes the document in the United States: on AWS in Ohio, with Microsoft Azure (East US) for speech and text recognition, Mathpix for mathematics, and Google Gemini through OpenRouter for picture descriptions.
  • No training. Pneuma never uses documents to train AI.
  • How long it is kept. Your document stays in your Scribe account under your retention setting: 30 days unless you changed it, 1 to 30 days by choice, or as long as you like with a subscription. An organization sets 1 to 30 days. QUILL cannot read or change this setting. Change it on the Scribe website with Open My Scribe Account in Browser.
  • Picture descriptions. Pictures can be described by AI. Pneuma is reviewing its picture-description provider, and makes no promise yet about how long that provider keeps data.
  • Deleting. Open My Scribe Documents, choose Move to Trash, then in the Trash choose Delete Forever. Something left in the Trash is deleted after 7 days.

And on your own computer:

  • Your password stays with Scribe. You sign in on Scribe’s own page in your browser. QUILL keeps only the sign-in, in Windows’ secure store.
  • A PDF password is used once. It is never saved or written to a log.
  • The log is bare. QUILL’s log holds only what stage a job reached, its document ID and its page count. Never your document’s text, its title when the name looks private, your sign-in or a password.
  • Your results are yours. QUILL saves them in your own folders. The queue and the downloads belong to your Windows account, and are never shared with anyone else who uses this computer.
  • Consent every time. Each upload needs Send and Convert. There is no “don’t ask again”. The one standing consent is a watch folder profile’s, which you give, can see in the profile, and can take back.

Copyright

Convert only documents you own, have permission for, or that are out of copyright. The Convert dialog says so every time.

Safe Mode

Safe Mode turns every Scribe command off. Choose one and you hear “Scribe for Documents is disabled in Safe Mode. Restart QUILL without –safe-mode to use network features.” Nothing is queued, and the scanned-PDF question does not offer Scribe. Jobs already sent carry on at Scribe, and the next normal start picks them up.

Troubleshooting

When something goes wrong, you hear one sentence that names Scribe for Documents and the file, and usually Scribe’s own reason. Scribe writes its messages for people to read. QUILL adds a code, such as QUILL-SCRIBE-PAGES, which you never hear. It is in the Details box and in Copy Details, for when you ask for help.

Sign-in has ended (QUILL-SCRIBE-SIGNIN)

You hear: “Your Scribe for Documents sign-in has ended. Conversions are paused until you sign in again.”

Why: your sign-in ran out, or was taken back on the Scribe website.

What to do: in Scribe Conversions, press Sign In… on the job. Once you are signed in again, the job carries on.

Sign-in could not reach QUILL (QUILL-SCRIBE-SIGNIN-LOCAL)

You hear: “No port could be opened on this computer to receive the sign-in. Check that security software allows QUILL apps to listen on 127.0.0.1.” Or, when the browser did not open, that QUILL could not open your browser.

Why: QUILL waits for Scribe’s answer on this computer only, at 127.0.0.1. Security software blocked that, or no browser opened.

What to do: allow QUILL in your security software, then Try Again. If the browser did not open, press Copy Sign-in Link, paste it into any browser, and sign in there. QUILL is still waiting.

Not enough pages (QUILL-SCRIBE-PAGES)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents could not convert Notes.pdf:” then Scribe’s message, then “Use Buy Pages in Scribe Conversions, then Retry.”

Why: your account does not have enough pages.

What to do: press Buy Pages, buy pages in your browser, come back and press Retry. Without pages, Scribe still makes a free preview of the first two pages; you can read it in your account on the Scribe website.

PDF needs its password (QUILL-SCRIBE-PASSWORD)

You hear: “Notes.pdf needs its password. Open Scribe Conversions to enter it.” After three wrong tries: “Scribe for Documents could not convert Notes.pdf: Notes.pdf was not sent: password not accepted”.

Why: the PDF is locked, and the password was missing or wrong.

What to do: press Enter Password… on the job, as in A password-protected PDF. After three tries, press Retry to start again.

Scribe asked QUILL to slow down (QUILL-SCRIBE-BUSY)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents asked QUILL to slow down; trying again in a minute.”

Why: Scribe allows each person only so many actions a minute, and your own use of the Scribe website counts too.

What to do: nothing. QUILL waits a minute and carries on. The Details box says when it will try again.

Not a file Scribe takes (QUILL-SCRIBE-NOTDOC)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents cannot convert Data.xml: it takes PDF, pictures, Office, OpenDocument, text, EPUB and MOBI files, not web pages.” For a web page: “page.html: Scribe for Documents does not take web pages. Save it as Word or PDF first.”

Why: Scribe takes only the 23 kinds of file in What you can send.

What to do: save the file as Word or PDF, and send that. For a page you are working on in QUILL, use Convert This Document, which sends it as Word.

File over 500 MB (QUILL-SCRIBE-TOOBIG)

You hear: “Video.pdf is 612 MB. Scribe for Documents takes files up to 500 MB.”

Why: the file is bigger than Scribe takes. Nothing was sent.

What to do: split the document into parts, or save a smaller copy with smaller pictures.

No longer in your Scribe account (QUILL-SCRIBE-GONE)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents could not convert Notes.pdf:” and Scribe’s reason. The row says “No longer in your Scribe account”.

Why: the document was deleted on Scribe, perhaps on the website or because its time ran out.

What to do: press Remove from List. To have it again, start a new conversion from the file.

Connection lost (QUILL-SCRIBE-OFFLINE)

You hear: after a few seconds without a connection, “Reconnecting to Scribe for Documents; your conversion is still running there.” During an upload: “Upload of Notes.pdf was interrupted; QUILL will resume it.”

Why: your internet connection dropped, or Scribe could not be reached.

What to do: nothing, usually. The conversion carries on at Scribe, and QUILL reconnects by itself without uploading again. You hear nothing when it comes back. If you were signing in or loading a list, you may hear “Couldn’t connect to Scribe. Check your connection and try again.”

Could not save the result (QUILL-SCRIBE-SAVE)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents could not convert Notes.pdf:” then, for example, that the folder is not writable or could not be created.

Why: the download folder is full, read-only, or missing.

What to do: free some space, or choose another Download folder in Options, then press Retry. What Scribe made is still in your Scribe account, so nothing is lost and no more pages are used.

Off in Safe Mode (QUILL-SCRIBE-OFF)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents is disabled in Safe Mode. Restart QUILL without –safe-mode to use network features.”

Why: QUILL is running in Safe Mode.

What to do: restart QUILL normally.

Conversion failed (QUILL-SCRIBE-FAILED)

You hear: “Scribe for Documents could not convert Notes.pdf:” and Scribe’s reason, or “Something went wrong. Please try again.”

Why: Scribe could not finish the work, or answered in a way QUILL did not expect.

What to do: press Retry. If it fails again, press Copy Details and send that to support with Help > Get Help from Support.

Codes you will not hear

QUILL-SCRIBE-IN-PROGRESS, QUILL-SCRIBE-NOT-COMPLETE and QUILL-SCRIBE-CANCELLED are used inside QUILL. Scribe runs one format of a document at a time, so QUILL simply waits; and Cancel Waiting says its own sentence instead.

Questions people ask

Does QUILL send my document anywhere without asking?

No. Every upload needs Send and Convert. The only exception is a watch folder profile where you checked the consent box yourself.

Can I stop a conversion?

Scribe cannot be stopped once it has started. Cancel Waiting stops QUILL waiting and downloading, and says so: “QUILL stopped waiting for Notes.pdf. Scribe will still finish it and may use pages; it stays in My Scribe Documents.”

Will a second format cost more pages?

No. Pages are charged once for each document. Use Download Formats… in My Scribe Documents.

Is Scribe the same as QUILL’s AI?

No. Scribe is a document service with its own consent. It does not go through Choose Your AI. Turning AI off in QUILL only changes whether Describe the pictures starts checked.

Where are my files?

In Documents\Scribe for Documents, in a folder for each document, unless you chose another folder in Options. Open Folder in Scribe Conversions takes you there.

Can I convert a file straight from Explorer?

Yes, if you checked Add “Convert with Scribe for Documents” to Send To in QUILL’s installer. See From Explorer: Send To.

Can I use QUILL Lite at the same time?

Yes. They share one sign-in, one queue and one Options window. Only one sends at a time, and only the one running the job speaks.

Why did nothing open?

A result opens by itself only when Options says Open it, and the app that asked is in front. Tagged PDF and the offline web page are only saved. Use Open Result or Next Steps….

What happens if QUILL closes during a conversion?

Nothing is lost. The next time QUILL starts, it downloads what was made and tells you.
