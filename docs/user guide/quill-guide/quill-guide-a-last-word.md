# A last word

The best way to get to know QUILL is to use it on something real: a note you
care about, a PDF you scanned and need to check, an EPUB chapter you want to
find your way around, a Markdown file that needs tidying, or a web page you
want to make easier to use.

I would like QUILL to feel like a good guide sitting beside you while you
work, never one standing in your way. If it does its job, you will spend less
time wondering what it can do and more time on what you want to write next.

## Acknowledgments

QUILL stands on the shoulders of the screen-reader-first editors that came
before it. The keyboard-first way of comparing documents, and selecting with
`F8` from an anchor, were inspired by EdSharp and Boxer. I am grateful to
those projects and the people who made them, for showing what an accessible,
keyboard-driven editor can be.
