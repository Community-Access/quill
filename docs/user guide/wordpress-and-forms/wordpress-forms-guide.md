# WordPress and Forms in QUILL and QUILL Lite

This is the manual for two things QUILL and QUILL Lite share: looking after
WordPress websites, and writing accessible forms. Everything in it works the
same way in both editors. It is a book of its own, beside each editor's user
guide, because there is a lot to say and you may only need part of it.

You write in the editor, and you look after your sites in the WordPress
Workbench, a window of its own for every site you have connected. The
chapters follow that order: connect a site, learn the Workbench, write and
publish, then look after everything else.

## How to read this manual

- **New to it?** Read Chapters 1, 2 and 3, in that order. They take you from
  nothing to a published post, and show you round the Workbench on the way.
  Then dip into the rest as you need it.
- **Looking after a site?** Chapters 5 to 10 cover pictures, comments,
  people, menus, checking, running the site, moving content, and the plugins
  QUILL works with.
- **Only want forms?** Start at Chapter 11. Chapters 12 and 13 cover putting
  a form on a site and looking after it there, which needs Chapter 1's
  connection first.
- **Looking something up?** Chapter 16 has every key, and Chapter 17 lists
  every place, menu and command in the Workbench. Appendix A lists the
  WordPress features QUILL uses and what your site needs for each.
- **Something went wrong?** Chapter 15 has the sentences QUILL says when
  something fails, and what to do about each.

Every chapter starts with why you are there, what you will do and what you
will have at the end, and ends with links to the chapters either side and
back to these contents. Headings are real headings, so your screen reader's
heading keys move through each chapter.

Throughout the manual, "Community Radio" stands for your own site's name,
which QUILL says in its place. The guided lessons in **Help > Tutorials**,
in both editors, walk through the commonest tasks on a test site, and tell
you what you should hear at each step.

## Keys at a glance

- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Grave**, then a letter: the WordPress keys. Grave is the
  key below Escape. O opens a page or post, P publishes, H opens the
  WordPress Workbench, M opens the Media Library, S switches site, W opens a
  page by its web address, D saves a draft to the site, and F opens form
  Responses. Escape leaves without doing anything.
- **Ctrl+S** saves on your computer only. It never sends anything to a site.
- **Ctrl+Shift+P** opens the Command Palette, which finds every WordPress and
  Forms command by name.
- **Ctrl+Z** takes back any change a WordPress or Forms command made to your
  document. In the Workbench, it takes back the last change made to a site.
- **F1** on any control says what it is for.

The other Forms commands have no keys of their own: use the menus or the
Command Palette. [Chapter 16](wordpress-forms-16-keys.html) has every key.
