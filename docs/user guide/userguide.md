# QUILL User Guide

This is the guide to QUILL, the writing studio for Windows and macOS made for
people who use a screen reader. It is written for QUILL 1.0.0. QUILL is still
in beta, so now and then the guide may describe something your copy does not
have yet.

Each chapter is a page of its own. This page is the contents: every chapter,
two sentences on what it covers, and links to its main topics, so you can go
straight to the part you need.

## How to use this guide

If you are new to QUILL, start with
[Chapter 1: Your first session](quill-guide/quill-guide-01-your-first-session.html)
and read on from there. Otherwise, find the chapter for the job in front of
you below, or go straight to one of its topics.

- On this page, each chapter is a level 3 heading. Press 3 in JAWS or NVDA to
  move from chapter to chapter, and Tab or the down arrow to hear its topics.
- Every chapter page starts with the chapter's title as a level 1 heading. Its
  main topics are level 2 headings, and their parts are level 3 and below.
- At the bottom of every chapter are links to the chapter before it, the one
  after it, and back to these contents.
- In QUILL, **Help > Open User Guide** (`Ctrl+Shift+F1`) opens this page. The
  Full details link in F1 help opens the chapter it is about, at the right
  place.
- If you would rather read the whole guide as one book, `userguide.epub`, in
  the docs folder beside QUILL, has every chapter in it, in order.
