Suggest a station or podcast

Community Picks is the list Quill Radio and QUILL Cast offer from their Community menu. Tell us about something worth adding.

Already using Quill Radio? You do not need this page at all — Community > Suggest a Station or Podcast… sends it for you, with no account and without leaving the app.

What are you suggesting?



What it should be called in the list.


The stream address for a station, or the feed address for a podcast. Either https:// or http:// is fine — plenty of community stations are http-only, and we would rather list them.


One or two sentences for somebody who has never heard it. This is what people read when they are deciding, so plain and specific beats promotional.


Such as en, or en-US. Optional.


Such as ACB Media, or Reading Services. Optional — leave it to us if you are unsure.


Anything that would help us decide. Not published.

What happens next

Sending opens your suggestion as an issue on GitHub, already filled in — press Submit new issue there to finish. A person reads every one. Approved suggestions are published within a day and appear in everybody's app the next time it refreshes, with no update required.

That last step needs a GitHub account. If you do not have one and do not want one, Quill Radio can send the whole thing for you: Community > Suggest a Station or Podcast… needs no account and never leaves the app.

Other ways

  • Fill in the GitHub issue form directly.
  • Use Community > Suggest a Station or Podcast… inside Quill Radio.
