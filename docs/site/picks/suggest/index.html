Suggest a station or podcast

Community Picks is the list Quill Radio and QUILL Cast offer from their Community menu. Tell us about something worth adding.

No account, and no sign-in — not here and not anywhere else. Fill in the form on this page, press Send suggestion, and it reaches us directly.

If you are already in Quill Radio, the Community menu, then Suggest a Station or Podcast, does exactly the same thing without leaving the app. Quill Radio is free to download.

What are you suggesting?


This decides what kind of address we expect below.


What it should be called in the list.


The stream address for a station, or the feed address for a podcast. Either https:// or http:// is fine — plenty of community stations are http-only, and we would rather list them.


One or two sentences for somebody who has never heard it. This is what people read when they are deciding, so plain and specific beats promotional. Up to about 600 characters.


Such as en, or en-US. Optional, up to 16 characters.


Such as ACB Media, or Reading Services. Optional, up to 64 characters — leave it to us if you are unsure.


Anything that would help us decide. Optional, and not published.

What happens next

Your suggestion reaches us as soon as you press the button. A person reads every one. Approved suggestions are published within a day and appear in everybody's app the next time it refreshes, with no update required.

Other ways to send a suggestion

  • In Quill Radio: the Community menu, then Suggest a Station or Podcast. No account, and it never leaves the app.
  • Fill in the GitHub issue form on github.com. This one does need a free GitHub account.
