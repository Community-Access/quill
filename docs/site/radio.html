Quill Radio: the next generation of the ACB Radio Tuner.

For over a decade, the ACB Radio Tuner gave blind and low-vision listeners a small, devoted way to hear ACB Media on the desktop. Quill Radio carries that legacy forward - every ACB Media stream plays the moment you install, with zero setup - and then goes further: tens of thousands of stations from every country, a real nested favorites system, recording that survives a dropped connection, a wake-up timer, hardware media keys, and a system tray presence that keeps the music going while you work. Free. Built the way a screen reader user would build it.

Version 3.0.4, released 2026-09-28. Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. Two downloads, and everything is bundled in both - FFmpeg for recording and the mpv playback engine included. The installer is the one most people want: it sets up the shared QuillVille Runtime if it is not already there, then Quill Radio, with a Start Menu entry and an uninstaller. The portable ZIP unpacks anywhere, a USB stick included, and writes nothing to the computer it runs on: settings, favorites, history, recordings and downloads live beside QuillRadio.exe from the very first launch. Help > Check for Updates (Ctrl+Alt+U) offers whichever of the two you are running.

Both files are digitally signed and are on the Quill Radio 3.0.4 release page, with their SHA-256 checksums.

What's new in 3.0

  • Two downloads. An installer and a portable ZIP. A portable copy writes nothing to the computer it runs on, and Check for Updates (Ctrl+Alt+U) offers the one you are running, with the release's notes to read first.
  • Browse is a place, not a list. Browse Stations (Ctrl+B) is one tree: By Country, By Language, Trending Now and Recently Added, radio reading services, whole libraries - the Internet Archive, LibriVox, Project Gutenberg, Audius, Mixcloud, ccMixter, AudioPub - Apple and Podcast Index podcasts with no account, SHOUTcast, Live365, Radio Paradise in lossless FLAC, your own Icecast servers, YouTube, and television from iptv.org. Station > Choose Browse Sources turns off any branch you never use, and an off branch is never contacted.
  • A radio that works before the internet does. More than 62,000 stations ship inside the app, so browsing and searching answer instantly, and still answer with no connection at all.
  • Search that finds the station you meant. Search Stations (Ctrl+F) understands a brand, a frequency, a call sign and a place - "Sunny 105.7 Gulf Shores Alabama" finds the station.
  • Live radio you can rewind. Rewind 30 Seconds (Ctrl+Shift+Left) and Back to Live (Ctrl+Shift+L) on any live station. Pause (Ctrl+Space) is for podcasts, recordings and files; a live broadcast keeps going, so it rewinds rather than pauses.
  • A timeline for anything with an end. Chapters, Go to Position (Ctrl+Alt+J), Where Am I (Ctrl+Shift+W), Play Faster and Slower, Skip Silence, and transcripts you can read and search.
  • Described audio, named. Audio > Audio and Described Audio (Ctrl+Shift+A) lists a video's audio tracks with the described one first, and Play Described Audio (Ctrl+Alt+D) switches straight to it.
  • Every window reaches the player. Browse, Search, Recordings, Favorites and the Player are real windows; Ctrl+Tab moves between them, Go To (Ctrl+G) reaches every place in the app, every menu item names its key, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last destructive action.
  • Your favorites on Alt+1 to Alt+0. The first ten favorites play with one key, without opening anything.
  • The app teaches itself. F1 explains any window and the control you are on, and Help > Tutorials (Ctrl+Alt+F1) opens 41 guided lessons that can run a step for you and move on when they notice you have done it.

Where this came from

Quill Radio is offered with real gratitude to the American Council of the Blind and everyone who built and maintained the original ACB Radio Tuner. That project proved something worth proving: a radio app designed specifically for blind listeners, by people who understood exactly what that meant, could be simple, dependable, and genuinely loved. Quill Radio doesn't replace that legacy - it's an attempt to honor it, by taking the same care and pointing it at the whole dial.

Everything you'd expect - and everything you didn't have before

From ten streams to the whole dial

The bundled ACB Media directory carries all ten ACB streams - Mainstream, The Trove, Cafe, Community, Live, Special, and the rest - playable the moment you install. Browse Stations (Ctrl+B) opens a tree of more than 62,000 stations worldwide, kept on your computer by the Station Catalog, by country, language, genre and more, alongside podcasts, audiobooks and television. Find Streams from a Website scans a page you give it for stream links, and you can always paste a URL directly. However a station reaches you, one keystroke makes it a favorite.

Favorites that grow the way your listening does

Folders as deep as you want - News, then News > Morning, then News > Morning > Local - created wherever you need them with New Folder (Ctrl+Shift+E), before a single station lives there. Rename any station to what you call it; your name follows it everywhere. Delete a folder and its stations step safely back to the top level - nothing vanishes with it. Every favorite remembers its own volume, because stations are mastered wildly differently and you should only have to fix that once.

Recording that survives the real internet

If your connection drops mid-recording, ffmpeg first rides out the gap; if the drop is real, Quill Radio waits, reconnects, and resumes into a cleanly numbered part file - attempts and spacing entirely yours to tune. Record what's playing, or record a different station while you listen to something else - the recorder never needed the player. Schedule a show once, daily, or weekly, picking straight from your favorites. The Recordings window shows the whole life cycle live: recording now with its size growing, recorded and ready, or scheduled and waiting.

An appliance, when you want it to be one

Check Resume Last Station on Launch once and Quill Radio becomes exactly what a radio should be: something you turn on. Play Last Station (Ctrl+L) picks up where you left off with no navigation at all. The Wake-Up Timer is the sleep timer's twin - pick a favorite, pick a time, once or every day. Hardware media keys control playback system-wide, even from the tray, with a live now-playing tooltip.

Built to be heard

Everything Quill Radio does, it says out loud, through the same announcement engine that powers QUILL - accurate, and compatible with JAWS, NVDA, and Narrator without ever stealing focus. The Command Palette (Ctrl+Shift+P) puts every command behind one searchable list. What's Playing (Ctrl+T) speaks the current track or show title from the stream's own metadata, the instant you ask.

One data store, shared with QUILL

Installed on the same computer, Quill Radio shares favorites, history, recordings, schedules, and settings with QUILL and QUILL Cast. Favorite a station here, and it's already a favorite in QUILL. Nothing is ever set up twice. A portable copy keeps its own, inside its own folder.

Tell us what to build next

Quill Radio is free, and it grows from the people who use it. Help > Get Help from Support (Ctrl+Alt+F2), right inside the app, writes to support@community-access.org in seconds - no account or sign-up needed - stamped with Quill Radio's own version so we always know exactly what you were running. A person at Community Access reads every message and replies by email. Station and podcast suggestions go the same way. Every report shapes what ships next.
