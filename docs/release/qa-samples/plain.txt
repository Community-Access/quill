Field Notes

The lighthouse keeper logged the weather every morning at six o'clock. Wind from
the northwest, seas moderate, visibility four miles.

By noon the fog had rolled in and the horn ran on its two-minute cycle. Nothing
passed the point until the mail boat at half past three.
