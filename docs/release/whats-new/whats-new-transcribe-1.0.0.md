# What's new in QUILL Transcribe 1.0

QUILL Transcribe types what you say into the program you are using, with the same speech models and dictation as QUILL and QUILL Lite. Your speech stays on your computer unless you choose an internet model yourself.

## Dictating

- **Press Alt+Shift+Backslash in the field you want, and speak.** Each phrase is typed when you pause. Change the key in File > Start and Stop Key... (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D), where Record Key writes out the keys you press.
- Hold the key to talk, or say "Quill dictate", if you turn either on.
- **Your screen reader stays quiet**: no more "period" after every phrase. A soft tick plays while a slow phrase is worked on, and the words so far appear on a braille display.
- **Paused, never misplaced.** Move to another field and typing stops; what you say next is held until you resume or discard it.

## Commands and correcting

- Say "QUILL" before a command so it is never typed, such as "QUILL scratch that", or press F11 and say it.
- "Correct that", "replace Tuesday with Wednesday" and "delete last sentence" work on the words QUILL Transcribe typed.
- Dictation profiles (Ctrl+Shift+F), Review Dictation (Shift+F4) and Dictation Status (Alt+F9).

## Models

- **Recommended for this computer** names the model that suits your computer, and why, from tests with recordings of real people.
- The built-in Moonshine and Whisper models need no download and no internet; between them they take English and Spanish. Ten larger models download from the Model Library (Ctrl+L) only when you ask.
- The model you choose is the one that runs. If it cannot run, you are told; nothing else is used without your say.
- OpenAI and twelve other speech providers can listen instead, with your own account. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

## Also in 1.0

- **Transcribe a Recording** turns an audio or video file into text in the background.
- **Dictate Here (Ctrl+F11)** in Quill Cast and Quill Radio dictates into the box you are in.
- Your dictation settings can be shared with QUILL, QUILL Lite and Quill Inkwell. Recent Phrases (Shift+F11) keeps words that could not be typed.
- It never types into password fields or into programs running as administrator, and recordings of your voice are never kept.
- Help > What's New (Ctrl+Shift+F1) opens the full changelog in your browser, and Help > Release Channel... chooses Stable, Beta or Dev.
