# What's new in Quill Converter 1.0

Quill Converter changes a sound or video file's format on your own computer, with nothing uploaded, and it was built for screen readers from the first line. This is its first public release.

## Converting

- **Add files with Alt+A**, or copy them in File Explorer and press Ctrl+V. Choose a format in Convert to (Alt+T) and press Ctrl+Enter. Your original is never changed.
- **34 formats out, 82 in**, each spoken with what it is for, such as "M4B audiobook". The list only offers what this computer can really write.
- **Video stays video**: MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV and more, with presets named for the result. A film's described-audio track comes through.
- **Convert with Quill Converter** on the File Explorer right-click menu puts every file you selected into one queue.
- **Convert from URL (Ctrl+U)** takes a video, a playlist or a channel.

## Hear it first

- **Effects** are named for the problem they solve: Clean up speech, Podcast ready, Audiobook ACX ready, Night listening and more. Custom Effects (Ctrl+E) changes one thing at a time.
- **Preview (Ctrl+P)** plays fifteen seconds exactly as the result will sound, and Hear Original (Ctrl+Shift+P) plays them as they are now.

## Chapters

- **Chapter marks (Alt+K)** keeps each file's chapters, reads a chapter list you wrote beside the file, finds them at the pauses, or adds one every so many minutes.
- **Join into One File (Ctrl+J)** makes a folder of chapters one audiobook, and Split by Chapters (Ctrl+Shift+S) goes the other way.
- **The Chapter Workbench (Ctrl+H)** renames, splits and merges chapters with a player.

## AI, if you want it

- A new AI menu (Alt+I) does nothing until you use it. Choose Your AI (Ctrl+Shift+F8) picks the free AI, your ChatGPT subscription or your own key.
- **Will This Play on My Device? (Ctrl+Shift+D)** asks which device and says what to convert to if the files will not play.
- **Make This Right for My Player... (Ctrl+F3)** starts one conversion from a sentence, after telling you exactly what it will do. It needs GitHub Copilot or Codex.
- The AI gets file details, never folders or the sound. Safe Mode turns the menu off.

## Also in 1.0

- **Reasons, not error codes.** The Conversion Report (Ctrl+R) says why a file failed in words you can act on.
- **File Properties (Alt+Enter)** says what is inside a file, and the drive it is on.
- **Process with Auphonic (Ctrl+Shift+A)** if you have an account. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
- Its windows stay sharp at 150 and 200 percent display scaling.
- Help > What's New (Ctrl+Shift+F1) opens the full changelog in your browser.
