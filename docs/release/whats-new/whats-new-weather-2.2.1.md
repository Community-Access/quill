# What's new in Quill Weather 2.2.1

Quill Weather now calls your places by the names you gave them, can tell you whether to go out, and keeps out of the way until you ask. Your saved places and settings come with you.

## Your places, by name

- **Save a place as Home or Summer Home**, and Current conditions, Quick Weather, the local time and the alert watch all use that name instead of a ZIP code or county. Leave the name empty and Weather uses the place name from the search.

## AI, when you ask for it

- **Should I Go Out? (Ctrl+Shift+G)** answers in plain words from the forecast for your primary place. Any active alert comes first, in the Weather Service's own words; the AI never rewords one.
- **Ask (Ctrl+Shift+8)** about the forecast. Your place's name goes with the question, never its coordinates.
- **Choose Your AI (Ctrl+Shift+F8)** picks the free AI, your ChatGPT subscription (Alt+F5) or your own key (Alt+F2). A line in the main window says which AI is in use.
- Text only, no radar or maps. The alert watch never uses AI, nothing runs on a timer, and Safe Mode turns every AI command off.

## Two complete editions

- **The installer** runs per user with no administrator prompt and brings the shared runtime it needs. **The portable zip** keeps its settings and places in its own data folder. Neither downloads anything on first launch.
- **Updates follow your edition**: Check for Updates offers a portable copy the portable zip and an installed copy the installer.
- A portable copy never adds a Windows startup entry or a background task. Install Weather if you want those.

## Finding your way

- **Help > Tutorials (Ctrl+Alt+F1)**: eleven guided lessons, five of them about the alert watch.
- **Help > Command Palette (Ctrl+Shift+P)** finds any Weather command by name.
- **Activity (Shift+F9)** lists recent results, and **F9** repeats the last one.
- **Help > What's New (Ctrl+Shift+F1)** opens the full changelog in your browser.

## Also in 2.2.1

- **No system-wide key by default.** Choose a show and hide key under File > Show and Hide Key if you want one.
- **App Sounds Play On (Ctrl+Shift+S)** sends Weather's own sounds to any sound card or nowhere; Alt+Shift+M turns them off and on.
- Sharp text at 150 and 200 percent display scaling, and the Weather Center's status line has a spoken name.
- A sun-and-cloud icon tells Weather apart from Radio, and QUILL Transcribe is on the QuillVille menu.
