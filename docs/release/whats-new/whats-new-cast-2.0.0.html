What’s new in QUILL Cast 2.0

QUILL Cast 2.0 is one window with everything in it, every list works from the keyboard, and when something happens you would want to know about, it tells you in a sentence. Your podcasts, folders, place in every episode, notes and settings carry on as they were.

Before you update: what works differently

  • Cast is one window. Podcasts is Ctrl+Shift+S, the Play Queue Ctrl+Shift+Q, Now Playing Ctrl+2, and Go To (Ctrl+G) lists every place.
  • Follow, not subscribe. The Subscriptions menu is now Podcasts, and Unsubscribe is Unfollow.
  • Keys that moved: show and hide Cast from anywhere is Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F12, Restore from a Backup Ctrl+Alt+F12, Mark as Played and Next Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Down, Favorites Alt+Shift+V and Playlists Alt+Shift+Y.
  • Save and Close instead of OK and Cancel in windows that change settings, so you can try something and adjust it.

Finding and following

  • Add Podcast (Ctrl+N): type a name, hear what a podcast is about, and follow it with Alt+O. Follow ACB Media Podcasts (Ctrl+Alt+B) follows the whole directory at once.
  • Folders: Copy to Folder (Ctrl+Shift+Y) puts a podcast in a second folder without following it twice; Ctrl+Shift+V moves it.
  • Enter on a podcast opens its list on the episode you last had selected.

Listening

  • Ctrl+Right skips ahead 30 seconds, Ctrl+Left goes back 15, Ctrl+P pauses and picks up where you stopped.
  • QUILL Lineups (Ctrl+U): named lists of episodes, podcasts and anything Quill Radio can play, by hand or by rules, shared with Quill Radio.
  • Cast’s own sounds can play where you choose; Alt+Shift+M turns them off and on.
  • Slider Keys in Preferences: Up is more (Home is 100 percent), or the Windows slider keys, for every Quill app at once. Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down are not changed.

Big libraries, kept safe

  • Refresh All has its own progress window, and Stop remaining checks cancels only the feeds that have not started.
  • A feed check that finishes late never undoes what you did meanwhile, and Ctrl+Z after Unfollow puts a podcast back with its queue, playlists and folders.
  • Saving, opening Downloads and checking disk space no longer hold up the window, even with thousands of podcasts.

Also new

  • Dictate Here (Ctrl+F11) dictates into a search box through QUILL Transcribe.
  • Connect to Spotify (Ctrl+Alt+S) and Browse Spotify Podcasts (Ctrl+Alt+V) are on the Help menu.
  • A portable copy updates in place, and a failed update puts back the version you had.
  • Sharp text at 150 and 200 percent display scaling; show notes links follow a high contrast theme.

Everything that changed: Help > What’s New (Ctrl+Shift+F1). The full release notes: Help > Release Notes (Ctrl+Alt+R). Tutorials: Ctrl+Alt+F1. Stuck? Help > Get Help from Support.
