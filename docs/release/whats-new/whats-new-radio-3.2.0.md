# What's new in Quill Radio 3.2

You can now ask the radio about what you are hearing, keep your own music and audiobooks beside your stations, and find out everything the radio has told you. Your favorites, settings and recordings come with you.

## Before you update: one last time by hand

- **On Quill Radio 3.0.4, Install and restart closes the radio without installing.** The fix is in 3.2.0, so update by hand this once: download the Quill Radio 3.2.0 installer from https://www.quillforall.org/radio.html and run it, or unzip the 3.2.0 portable zip over your portable folder. Every update after this one installs itself.
- There was never a public 3.1; 3.2.0 follows 3.0.4.

## Ask the radio

- **Ask QUILL Radio (Ctrl+Shift+8)** answers questions about the station and the song playing, using your ChatGPT plan (Community > Use My ChatGPT Subscription, Alt+F5).

## Your own music, YouTube and podcasts

- **Local Media (Ctrl+O)** plays your music and audiobooks, with playlists from other players.
- **Search YouTube (Ctrl+Shift+6)** for videos, playlists and channels; read comments, follow channels, hear about new videos, join live chat and skip sponsor segments.
- **QUILL Lineups (Ctrl+U)**: named lists of stations, episodes, videos and files, by hand or by rules, shared with QUILL Cast.
- Apple's podcast categories are full lists now, and the radio can tell you when your podcasts have new episodes.

## Recording

- Every recording ends with one message: the station, when it started, how long it is and where it is.
- The station advert a recording starts with is cut out for you.
- Split a recording into one file per song in Recording Settings (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+I).

## Knowing what happened

- **Notifications (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F3)** lists everything the radio has told you. **Activity (Shift+F9)** keeps results with what to do next, and **F9** repeats the last one.

## Your stations and settings

- **181.FM and laut.fm** are new in Browse Stations (Ctrl+B), and Networks > Music adds 181.FM, Big R Radio and 1.FM.
- **Double Tap Live**, a new station made for this community.
- **Note to Self** on any station (Shift+F10): you hear it when you arrow onto the station. Add your own tags, and find a station by the team it carries.
- **Find a setting** in Preferences (Ctrl+,) as you type. Radio's own sounds can play on the same sound card as the station; Alt+Shift+M turns them off and on.
- **Slider Keys** in Preferences: Up is more, with Home as 100 percent, or the Windows slider keys. One choice for every Quill app.
- **Help > What's New** (Ctrl+Shift+F1) opens the full list of changes.

## Fixed

Start with Windows works again, live stations stay close to live after a hiccup, Preferences opens at once, and backups name anything they leave out.

The full release notes: Help > Release Notes (Shift+F1). Stuck? Help > Get Help from Support (Ctrl+Alt+F2).
