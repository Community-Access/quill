# Quill Converter 1.0: every sound and video format, from the keyboard

**September 28, 2026 -- from Community Access**

**Right-click a file, choose Convert with Quill Converter, press Ctrl+Enter.
Hear how far it has got, hear when it is done, and hear why if anything went
wrong.**

Somewhere between "I recorded this in the wrong format" and "my player will not
open this," almost everybody hits the wall of media conversion. The usual answer
is a website that wants your recording uploaded to a stranger, or a program
whose buttons a screen reader cannot name.

**Quill Converter** is the other answer. It is a free converter for Windows,
part of the QuillVille family, built for screen readers from the first line.
It runs entirely on your own computer, and nothing you convert ever leaves it.

---

## What it does

- **34 formats out, 82 in.** 25 sound formats and 9 video formats, from MP3,
  M4A and M4B audiobooks to FLAC, Opus, WAV, MP4, MKV and WebM. Every format is
  spoken with what it is for -- "M4B audiobook -- remembers your place in book
  players" -- so you choose by purpose, not by acronym.
- **Fast video.** H.264 and H.265 are encoded on your graphics chip when it
  can, several times faster than the processor alone.
- **Video, with every track that matters.** A film's described-audio track and
  its second language come through, and MKV keeps the subtitles. Video presets
  are named for the result: Same quality, Phones and the web, Smaller file,
  Smallest file, and Change the container only.
- **Effects named for the problem.** Clean up speech, Podcast ready, Audiobook
  ACX ready, Louder dialogue for films and TV, Night listening and more. You
  should not need to know what a de-esser is to fix sibilance.
- **Hear it first.** Preview (Ctrl+P) plays fifteen seconds exactly as the
  result will sound; Hear Original (Ctrl+Shift+P) plays the same fifteen
  seconds untouched.
- **Chapters, kept.** A file's own chapter marks survive every conversion, and
  Quill Converter can make them for you -- at the pauses, every few minutes, or
  from a list you wrote. The Chapter Workbench (Ctrl+H) edits them by ear.
- **Join and split.** Join into One File (Ctrl+J) makes one audiobook from a
  folder of tracks, each track a chapter. Split by Chapters (Ctrl+Shift+S) goes
  the other way.
- **A video, a whole playlist, or a channel.** Paste a YouTube link and choose
  how much to take; every video's audio arrives in order, tagged as one album,
  and next time only the new ones download.
- **Edit Tags** (Ctrl+T): title, artist, album and cover art, without leaving
  the queue.
- **Right-click in File Explorer.** Select fifty files, choose Convert with
  Quill Converter once, and all fifty arrive in one window, in one queue.

## It tells you what is happening

A conversion is not silence until it ends. Progress is counted inside each
file, so a two-hour audiobook moves from 0 to 100 percent just as a queue of
fifty songs does. It is spoken at 25, 50 and 75 percent, and the status bar,
the progress bar and the tray icon's tooltip say how far it has got and about
how long is left: "Converting Book.m4b: 42 percent, about 3 minutes left".

**Stop means stop.** Ctrl+Enter or the Stop button ends the conversion at
once -- the file in progress too -- and removes its unfinished output, so
nothing half-written is left behind.

When something fails, the reason is in words -- "This file is copy-protected
and cannot be converted", "The output drive is full" -- and **Conversion
Report** (Ctrl+R) lists every file with what happened to it, ready to paste
into an email.

## Everything is included

Nothing downloads the first time you use a feature. FFmpeg, the mpv playback
engine, the URL downloader and the tagging library are all inside both
downloads. Conversion never touches the network; only Check for Updates and
Convert from URL do, and only when you ask.

Two downloads, choose one:

- **The installer** -- adds a Start Menu entry, the File Explorer right-click,
  and an uninstaller. It installs the shared QuillVille runtime if the computer
  does not already have it.
- **The portable ZIP** -- unpack anywhere, a USB stick included. It keeps its
  settings in its own folder and writes nothing to the computer it runs on.

Press **F1** anywhere in Quill Converter to hear what the window is for and what
the focused control does. **Ctrl+F1** opens the full user guide.
