# QUILL Transcribe Tutorials

Welcome. There are 5 short lessons here, about 24 minutes in all, and you can take them in any order. Each one is a few small steps, and each step tells you what to press and what you should hear.

This page is the lessons written out, so you can read them anywhere. If you would rather be walked through, open **Help > Tutorials... (Ctrl+Alt+F1)** in QUILL Transcribe. It does a step for you when you ask, and moves on by itself once it hears you have done one.

The keys here are the ones QUILL Transcribe comes with. If you have changed a key, the lessons inside the app use your key; this page cannot know about the change.

## Dictating with QUILL Transcribe

From a first sentence in the Practice window to dictating, correcting and reviewing in any program.

### Your first dictation, in the Practice window

Open the Practice window, dictate a sentence into its own field, and take the recognition test, without typing into any other program.

*4 steps, about 5 minutes.*

1. **Open the Practice window.** Open Practice Dictation from the Dictation menu. It has a field of its own, so nothing you say goes anywhere else while you learn.
   - Keys: Ctrl+Shift+P
   - You should hear: "Practice Dictation", then "Practice field, edit".

2. **Start dictation in the practice field.** With the focus in the practice field, press Start Dictation Here. Your own start and stop key does the same while you are in the field.
   - Keys: Ctrl+F11
   - You should hear: A rising tone and "Dictation on".

3. **Say a sentence, then pause.** Say: this is my first dictation period. Then stop talking for a moment. The pause is what tells dictation the phrase is finished.
   - You should hear: Your words read back, and they are in the practice field.

4. **Take the recognition test.** Press Read This Sentence and read the sentence shown above it aloud. The field is cleared first, so only the sentence is scored.
   - Keys: Alt+T
   - You should hear: How many words were right, and any that were heard differently.
   - Worth knowing: Below 80 percent, try speaking a little slower, moving closer to the microphone, or a larger model in Choose Model.

You have dictated, and you know how well your microphone and model hear you. Next, dictate into a real program.

Next: Pause and resume in another program.

### Pause and resume in another program

Dictate into Notepad, move away to see dictation pause and keep your words, and send them on with Resume.

*3 steps, about 5 minutes.*

1. **Start in a field of your own choosing.** Open Notepad, put the focus in it, and press your start and stop key. Dictation writes into that field and only that field.
   - Keys: Ctrl+F11
   - You should hear: A rising tone and "Dictation on".

2. **Move away while you talk.** Switch to another window with Alt+Tab and say a sentence. Nothing is typed into the window you moved to; your words are held.
   - Keys: Alt+Tab
   - You should hear: "Paused", and how many phrases are held.

3. **Resume where you started.** Go back to Notepad and choose Resume. The held words are typed into the field you started in, and dictation carries on.
   - Keys: Ctrl+R
   - You should hear: The held words, typed into Notepad.
   - Worth knowing: Resume in This Field sends them to the field you switch to next instead; Discard Held Words throws them away.

A focus change never sends your words somewhere you did not choose.

Next: Commands with the command word.

### Commands with the command word

Say QUILL before a command so it is never typed as words, and use command mode for several commands in a row.

*3 steps, about 5 minutes.*

1. **Dictate something to correct.** In the practice field, start dictation and say a short sentence, then pause, so there is a phrase for a command to act on.
   - Keys: Ctrl+F11
   - You should hear: Your sentence read back.

2. **Take it back by voice.** Say: QUILL scratch that. The command word first is what makes it a command; scratch that on its own is typed as words here.
   - You should hear: A rising tone, then "Scratched" and your words.

3. **Give a command with the key.** Press Speak a Command, then say a command without the command word, such as caps on. Pressed twice, it starts command mode.
   - Keys: F11
   - You should hear: "Caps on."
   - Worth knowing: Say dictation mode, or wait, to go back to dictating.

Say QUILL what can I say for a short list of what works here.

Next: Snippets and clips by voice.

### Snippets and clips by voice

Insert a saved snippet or a Copy Tray clip by saying its name, as one phrase that scratch that takes back.

*3 steps, about 4 minutes.*

1. **Start dictation in the practice field.** Open the Practice window and start dictation there, so the snippet goes into a field where it does no harm.
   - Keys: Ctrl+F11
   - You should hear: "Dictation on".

2. **Insert a snippet by name.** Say: QUILL insert snippet, then the snippet's name. Snippets are the ones you saved in QUILL; the whole snippet is typed at once.
   - You should hear: The snippet's text read back.

3. **Paste a clip.** Say: QUILL paste clip one. The first slot of the Copy Tray is typed, and QUILL scratch that takes all of it back out.
   - You should hear: The clip's text read back.

Snippets and clips only insert; they never read the other program's text.

Next: Review before it is typed.

### Review before it is typed

Send what you dictate to Review Dictation first, change it there, and insert it when it reads right.

*3 steps, about 5 minutes.*

1. **Dictate into the practice field.** Start dictation in the practice field and say two sentences, pausing after each, so there is something worth reviewing.
   - Keys: Ctrl+F11
   - You should hear: Each sentence read back.

2. **Open the review.** Press Review Dictation. The words wait there, in a field you can read and edit, until you choose Insert or Discard.
   - Keys: Shift+F4
   - You should hear: "Review Dictation", then the review field.

3. **Insert when it reads right.** Change anything you like with the keyboard, then choose Insert. The words are typed into the field the session is locked to.
   - You should hear: The words, typed into the practice field.

Send dictation to review first, in More Dictation Settings, makes every phrase wait for you this way.
