Chapter 13: Every window, field and button

What this chapter covers

This chapter is a reference. It goes through every WordPress command in menu order and, for each one, says where it is, which WordPress roles can use it, every field, list and button in its window, what each one does on your site, and what you hear. The earlier chapters explain how to use these together; come here when you want to know exactly what a button does before you press it. The names are the ones on screen, without their underlined letters. Forms windows are described in full in Chapters 8 and 9.

How to read this chapter

Where. Every command is in the same menu in QUILL and QUILL Lite. Where a command has a WordPress key, it is given as the letter to press after Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Grave (Chapter 12).

Who. WordPress’s standard roles, from least to most: Subscriber, Contributor, Author, Editor, Administrator. QUILL asks your site what your account may actually do, so a site that changes its roles with a plugin is read correctly; the roles below are WordPress’s defaults. In short:

  • Contributors write and edit their own posts, but cannot publish or upload.
  • Authors also publish their own posts and upload files. They cannot write pages.
  • Editors also write and publish pages, edit everyone’s posts, moderate comments and manage categories and tags.
  • Administrators also run the site: settings, menus, widgets, users, plugins, themes, fonts and styles.

A window your role cannot use does not open; you hear one sentence, such as “Your account on Community Radio is an Author, which cannot manage users.”

The shared shapes. Most windows are one of five shapes, so their common parts are described once here:

  • A list window has the list, sometimes a Find field above it (type and press Enter to narrow the list), a read-only Details field saying more about the row you are on, one button per action, and Close. Enter runs the main action. A button that needs a row is dimmed until you are on one.
  • A choice window has one list and OK and Cancel. Enter chooses.
  • A question window has one field and OK and Cancel.
  • A confirmation names the site and what will change, and has a button named for the change, such as Move to Trash, and Cancel. Nothing is sent until you press it.
  • A report has a read-only Report field to read line by line, Copy All, and Close.

The site. A command that works on a whole site, not the page in front of you, asks Site first when you have more than one: a choice window with your default site selected.

File > WordPress

New Page and New Post

  • Where: File > WordPress > New Page… (key G) and New Post… (key N).
  • Who: a post, Contributor and up; a page, Editor and up.
  • The window:
    • Site: which of your sites it belongs to, for good;
    • Title: filled in by you; the theme shows it;
    • Slug: filled in from the title as you type;
    • Write in: Markdown or HTML, which cannot change later;
    • for a post, a Categories group and a Tags group of check boxes;
    • Create and Cancel.
  • On the site: nothing. The document is made on your computer.
  • You hear: “New post for Community Radio. Ctrl+S keeps it on this computer; nothing is sent until you choose Save Draft to Site or Publish.”

Open Page or Post

  • Where: File > WordPress > Open Page or Post… (key O).
  • Who: Contributor and up; you see what your role may edit.
  • The windows:
    • Show: a choice of Posts, Pages and any kind your site adds, such as Events;
    • Open from your site: a list window. The list is labelled Posts, Pages or Items; each row is the title, status and when it changed. Find by title or status narrows it. Buttons: Open, Move to Trash… and Restore from Trash;
    • Open as: “HTML, exactly as the site has it” or “Markdown, easier to read; anything Markdown cannot show is kept exactly”;
    • Unsaved Version, when the site has a newer autosave: “Yes, open the unsaved version” or “No, open the copy on the site”.
  • On the site: reads the list and the page. Move to Trash and Restore from Trash change the page’s status.
  • You hear: “Opened Opening Hours from Community Radio.” If you already have a working copy: “Opened your working copy of Opening Hours. Compare with Site shows what changed on the site since.”

Open by Web Address

  • Where: File > WordPress > Open by Web Address… (key W).
  • Who: Contributor and up.
  • The window: a question, Web address, with the clipboard’s address already in it.
  • On the site: finds the page by its address, and opens it as above.
  • You hear: the usual “Opened” sentence, or “That address is not on any of your connected sites.”

Open Local Draft

  • Where: File > WordPress > Open Local Draft… (key L).
  • Who: anyone; it reads only your computer.
  • The window: a choice, Drafts, newest first, each with its site.
  • You hear: “You have no local WordPress drafts yet.” when there are none.

Save Draft to Site

  • Where: File > WordPress > Save Draft to Site (key D). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: Contributor and up for posts; Editor and up for pages.
  • On the site: creates or updates the page as a draft, uploads any pictures from your computer first, and keeps the site’s copy in Previous Versions before replacing it.
  • You hear: “Draft saved to Community Radio. It is not public.” If the site’s copy changed since you opened it, the The Site Copy Changed window asks What now: Compare the two, Open the site’s copy, or Send mine anyway (which asks once more, Send Mine).

Publish or Update

  • Where: File > WordPress > Publish or Update… (key P). Called Submit for Review when your role cannot publish. Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: publishing posts, Author and up; pages, Editor and up; Contributors submit for review.
  • The window: a confirmation titled, for example, “Publish on Community Radio”, with the title and what Check Before Publishing found, and a Publish button, or Publish Anyway when there are findings.
  • On the site: publishes the page, or updates it, keeping a private or scheduled status as it was.
  • You hear: “Published on Community Radio. The address is on the clipboard.” or “Submitted for review on Community Radio.” For a new page, Add to a Menu asks: Yes, open Menus; Not this time; or No, and stop asking for that site.

Schedule Publish

  • Where: File > WordPress > Schedule Publish… (key E). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: Author and up for posts; Editor and up for pages.
  • The window: Date (year-month-day), Time, Time zone (your site’s first), and a Problem field at the top when the last try could not be used. Then a confirmation, Schedule. On a page already scheduled, What now: Change the schedule, or Publish now (Publish Now).
  • On the site: sets the page to publish at that time.
  • You hear: “Scheduled on Community Radio for” and the date and time.

Open on Site and Copy Address

  • Where: File > WordPress > Open on Site (key I) and Copy Address (key Y). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: anyone who can open the page.
  • You hear: “Address copied.” or “Draft address copied. It needs a WordPress sign-in to open.” Before the page is on the site: “This is not on Community Radio yet.”

Properties

  • Where: File > WordPress > Properties… (key R). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: whoever may edit the page.
  • The window: About (read only: site, address, author, last change, reading level); Title; Slug; Status (Draft, Pending review, Published, Private); Excerpt; for a post, the Categories and Tags check boxes, Allow comments, Keep at the top of the blog; Password to read it (optional); and any custom and plugin fields. OK and Cancel.
  • On the site: nothing until your next send.
  • You hear: “Kept with your copy: 2 categories, 3 tags. They go to the site when you next send.”

Custom Fields

  • Where: File > WordPress > Custom Fields…. Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: whoever may edit the page.
  • The window: one field per custom field your site registers for that kind of page, each labelled and explained by the site, and OK.
  • You hear: “3 custom fields kept with your copy. They go to Community Radio when you next send.” or “Community Radio has no custom fields for a post.”

Plugin Fields

  • Where: File > WordPress > Plugin Fields…. Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: whoever may edit the item.
  • The window: only the fields your site’s plugins have:
    • The Events Calendar: Starts, Ends, Venue;
    • WooCommerce: Regular price, Sale price, and Stock where the shop counts it;
    • Yoast SEO or Rank Math: SEO title and SEO description, each with a read-only count against what search engines show.
  • On the site: saves straight away; a price change asks first (Change Price).
  • You hear: “Saved to Community Radio:” and what was saved.

Previous Versions

  • Where: File > WordPress > Previous Versions… (key V). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: whoever may edit the page; the site’s own history needs the right to edit it there.
  • The window: a list window, Versions, newest first. Buttons: View… (a report, Version, of the whole text) and Restore.
  • On the site: nothing. Restore only changes your document.
  • You hear: “Version put in the editor. Nothing was sent; your text before is in Previous Versions.”

Compare with Site

  • Where: File > WordPress > Compare with Site (key C). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • The window: a report, lines starting “Site:” and “Yours:”.

Move to Trash

  • Where: File > WordPress > Move to Trash… (key T). Dimmed outside a WordPress page.
  • Who: whoever may delete the page: your own posts, Author and up; anyone’s, Editor and up.
  • The window: a confirmation, Move to Trash.
  • On the site: moves the page to WordPress’s Trash.
  • You hear: “‘Hours’ moved to the Trash on Community Radio.”

Send and Copy

All in File > WordPress > Send and Copy:

  • Send This Document to WordPress… Make it a: post or page. Then the site. The draft is made, its pictures uploaded. Who: Contributor and up (Author and up with pictures, which need uploading).
  • Publish to Another Site… Dimmed outside a WordPress page. Copy to: the other site. If that site lacks some categories or tags, a confirmation, Create Them, or Cancel to copy without them. Arrives as a draft.
  • Duplicate… Duplicate a: post or page; then the item; then Where: this site or another. You hear “‘Hours (copy)’ is a new draft on Community Radio.”
  • Send a Folder as Drafts… A folder chooser; then a confirmation, Send 3 Drafts, which lists anything to look at in each file.
  • Download My Writing… A folder chooser. Saving runs in the background, and you hear when it is done. Run it again to carry on or update.

Switch Site, Sites and Connect a Site

  • Switch Site… (key S): a choice, Site. You hear “Default site: Community Radio.”
  • Sites… (key Shift+S): the WordPress Sites list window. Buttons: Add…, Rename… (question, Name), Verify, Make Default, Remove… (confirmation, Remove), and Which Password, which says which of your application passwords QUILL signs in with.
  • Connect a Site… (key A): the Connect a WordPress Site window, with Kind of site, Site address, Username, Application password, Name for this site (optional), Get Password with My Browser, Connect and Cancel, and a Problem field after a failed try. Then, on older sites, Paste the Application Password, and sometimes Your WordPress Username. Any role can connect.

Edit > WordPress

All dimmed outside a WordPress page. Who: anyone editing the page.

  • Next Block and Previous Block: you hear the block, its place and its first words.
  • Where Am I: you hear the block and what it sits inside.
  • Hear Where a Link Goes: with no link at the caret, a question, Address; then a confirmation, Insert Link. Needs WordPress 5.9 or later.
  • Fix Link Text…: the Fix Link Text list window, Links, with Use the Page’s Title and Use All Titles. You hear “The link now says ‘About’.” or “3 links now say where they go.”

View

All dimmed outside a WordPress page.

  • WordPress Blocks… (key B): the Blocks list window. Buttons: Go To, Move Up, Move Down, Duplicate, Remove… (confirmation, Remove Block), Change To… (choice, Kind of block), Group, Ungroup. Changes only your document.
  • Preview Blocks: a report, as your site draws the blocks. Nothing is saved on the site.
  • Block Notes…: the Notes list window. Buttons: Go to It, Reply… (question, Reply), Resolve or Reopen, Add Note…. Needs WordPress 6.9 or later. Who: whoever may edit the page.
  • Play Media Here: opens the player (see Media Library, below).

Insert

All dimmed outside a WordPress page.

  • WordPress Block…: the Insert Block list window, Blocks, with Find, Insert, and WordPress.org Patterns… (question, Search for; choice, Patterns; confirmation, Insert and the pattern’s name). A block’s own form follows, with one field, check box or list per setting.
  • Picture from the Media Library…: a choice, Files; then the picture window. Who: Author and up.
  • Picture from This Computer…: a file chooser, then the picture window. Uploads on your next send. Who: Author and up.
  • The picture window: Alternative text, Suggest Alternative Text, Decorative: it adds nothing a visitor needs, Caption, Display width (choose or type pixels), File size (the library’s sizes), Use as featured image, Link to (Nothing, The picture file, or an address you type), Describe Image…, Insert and Cancel. You hear “Picture inserted:” and its alternative text.
  • Link to a Page on This Site…: a choice, Page or post. You hear “Link to ‘About’ inserted.”
  • Note on This Block…: a question, Note. In a Markdown document, a confirmation first, Add Note.
  • Make a Picture…: the Picture Maker: Describe the picture, or the change you want, Shape, Background, Colour (for a solid background), Make the picture (then Make the change), Suggest from Page Content, Versions, Description of the chosen version, Describe Image, Open in Image Viewer, Save As…, Insert into Document…, Open folder and Close. A confirmation, Make the Picture, names the provider before anything is sent.
  • WordPress Icon…: the Insert Icon list window, Icons, with Find, Insert with a Label… (question, Label) and Insert as Decorative. Needs WordPress 7.0 or later.

Format > WordPress

All dimmed outside a WordPress page.

  • Block Settings…: the block’s own form. In a Markdown document a confirmation may come first, Make Protected Block. You hear “Block settings changed.”
  • Image Size…: a choice, Size, with Back to Earlier Size at the top when there is one. You hear “Picture shown at” and the size.
  • Mark Language…: a question, Language. You hear, for example, “The selection is marked as French.”

Tools > WordPress: checking

  • Check Before Publishing… (key K): the list window, Things to look at, with Go to It. Dimmed outside a WordPress page. Reads only your document.
  • Check This Page: the list window, Findings, with Explain…, Go to It, Write a Note for the Theme Author…, Page Shape… and Dead Links…. Reads the public page; any role.
  • Check the Whole Site…: a choice, How thorough; then the list window, Problems, with Pages… and Fix in Bulk… (a preview report, then a confirmation such as Fix 17 Pages). Fixing needs the right to edit those pages: Editor and up.
  • Undo Site Fix…: the list window, Fixes, with Undo… and View….
  • Site-wide Find and Replace…: questions Find and Replace with; a choice, How to match (plain words or a pattern, any case or matching case); then the list window, Matches, with Include or Skip, Include All, Skip All, Include Block Settings and Replace… (confirmation, Replace in 4 Pages). Editor and up.
  • Write an Accessibility Statement…: a question, Contact. Opens a new page draft; nothing is sent.
  • Palette Report: a report. Any role.
  • In Short…: uses your AI; puts a paragraph at the top. Dimmed outside a WordPress page.

Tools > WordPress: pictures and media

  • Media Library… (key M): the list window, Files, with Find, Insert…, Play…, Used In, Attach to This Page or Post, Set as Featured Image, Suggest Alternative Text…, Describe Image…, Edit Alternative Text… (question, Alternative text), Copy Address and Remove from Library… (confirmation, Remove). Who: Author and up; changing or removing someone else’s file, Editor and up.
  • The player: Play and Pause, Stop, Rewind and Forward (ten seconds), Where Am I, Speed, Position, Volume, Mute, Captions, Go to Position…, Open in Quill Media Player, Open in Browser and Close.
  • Alt Text Review… (key X): the list window, Pictures, with Edit…, Mark Decorative, Suggest… and Go to Picture. Dimmed outside a WordPress page; changes only your document.
  • Alt Text for the Whole Library…: the list window, Pictures, with Write…, Suggest… and Save Now. Author and up for your own files; Editor and up for everyone’s.
  • Visual Review…: a confirmation, Send for Review, naming what goes to your AI; then a report. Published pages only.

More Media

All in Tools > WordPress > More Media. Uploading needs Author and up.

  • Upload Files… (key U): a file chooser; then for each file the upload window: Alternative text, Suggest Alternative Text, Decorative: it adds nothing a visitor needs, Title, Caption, Describe Image…, Upload and Cancel. If the file is already there, a confirmation, Use the One There.
  • Upload from Web Address…: a question, Address.
  • Paste Image: the upload window, for the copied picture.
  • Upload a Large File…: a file chooser. Needs a site that takes uploads in pieces; otherwise it says Upload Files sends it in one go.
  • Regenerate Sizes…: a choice, Picture. Needs WordPress 6.9 or later.
  • Make a Smaller Copy…: choices Picture, Largest width and Format; then a confirmation, Upload.
  • Edit a Picture on the Site…: choices Picture and Edit (turn, flip or crop, where the site offers it); a confirmation, Make Edited Copy; then Use It Here.
  • Site Logo…: the list window, Logo and icon, with From the Library…, From a File…, From the Picture Maker…, Preview a Width… and Restore Earlier…; then a confirmation naming the new picture. Administrator.
  • Preview and Review…: a choice, Preview a new width for; a question, Width in pixels; then as Visual Review.
  • Captions for Video…: a choice of video; the review question, Captions, one on each line with its time.
  • Transcript for Audio…: a choice of sound file; the review question, Transcript; a choice, Insert it: folded or in full.
  • Make a Listening Version…: dimmed outside a WordPress page. When one exists, a confirmation, Make Again.
  • Name Embeds: dimmed outside a WordPress page. A report.
  • Publish a Document as a Page…: a file chooser; a question, Page title; for a scanned file, a confirmation, Read the Pages. Editor and up, for the page.

Tools > WordPress: the site’s content

  • Comments…: the list window, Comments, with Show… (choice, Show: Waiting, Approved, Spam, Trash), the status’s own buttons (Approve, Unapprove, Spam, Not Spam, Trash, Restore), Approve All…, Reply…, Edit…, Open on Site, Waiting Count on First Use, Mark, Mark Everything and Do with Marked…. Editor and up.
  • Categories and Tags…: a choice, Manage; then the list window with Add…, Rename…, Merge Into…, Convert to Tag… (or Category), Find Unused…, Delete… and Make Default… (categories). Editor and up; Make Default, Administrator.
  • Menus…: a choice, Menu; then the list window, Menu items, with Add Page…, Add Post…, Add Category…, Add Link…, Rename…, Move Up, Move Down, Move In, Move Out, Remove…, Check Menu, Other Menu… and Locations…. Administrator.
  • Menu History…: a choice, Menu; the list window, Versions, with Bring Back Removed Items… and Restore This Version…. Administrator.
  • Site Check: a report. Administrator.
  • Site Settings…: the list window, Settings, with Change…: Site title, Tagline, Site language, Time zone, Date format, Time format, First day of the week, Administrator email, Front page shows, Front page, Posts page, Posts per page, New posts take comments, New posts take pingbacks, Turn emoticons into pictures, Default post format, Site icon. Each change is a confirmation such as Change Tagline. Administrator.

Tools > WordPress > Design

Block themes only. Administrator.

  • Style Variations…: the list window, Variations, with Apply… (confirmation) and Put Back the Previous Look….
  • Site Styles…: the list window, Styles, with Change… (question, Colour or Size) and Put Back the Previous Look….

Tools > WordPress > Plugin Extras

Only with the plugin on the site.

  • Events Coming Up…: the list window, Events, with Open and Open on Site.
  • New Event…: Name, Starts, Ends, Venue, and Make Draft.
  • Shop Orders…: the list window, Orders, read only, with Copy Order… (confirmation, Copy Order). Whoever the shop lets read its orders.

Tools > WordPress > Manage

  • Users…: the list window, Users, with Add User… (questions Username, Email, Name, a choice Role, and a confirmation), Edit…, Change Role…, Delete… (choice, Give their posts and pages to, then a confirmation), Application Passwords… and My Profile… (Display name, Biography, Website). Administrator; My Profile, every role.
  • Application Passwords…: the list window, Passwords, with Revoke…. Every role, for their own.
  • Plugins…: the list window, Plugins, with Activate or Deactivate…, Add Plugin… (question, Plugin’s WordPress.org name), Delete… and Open in Dashboard. Administrator.
  • Themes…: the list window, Themes, with Open in Dashboard. Administrator.
  • Fonts…: the list window, Fonts, with Install… (choice, Font) and Delete…. Administrator; WordPress 6.5 or later and a block theme.
  • Synced Patterns…: the list window, Patterns, with Edit…, Rename…, Unsync…, Delete… and New from Selection…; first a choice, Category, when the site sorts patterns. Contributor and up for patterns your role may change.
  • Widgets…: a choice, Widget area; the list window, Widgets, with Add Text…, Edit…, Move to Area…, Remove… and Open in Dashboard. Administrator; classic themes.
  • Site Map…: the list window, Site map, with Open and Open on Site.
  • Template Outline: a report. Block themes.
  • Bulk Edit…: a choice, Edit: Posts or Pages; the list window with Select, Select All, Change… (choices Change and To; then a confirmation) and Undo Bulk Edit…. Editor and up.
  • Page Order…: the list window, Pages, with Move Up, Move Down, Move In, Move Out and Open. Editor and up.
  • Previous Looks…: the list window, Looks, with Palette Report… and Go Back to This Look…. Administrator.
  • Space Used: a report. Administrator.
  • Download a Copy of the Site’s Design…: a save window. Administrator, block themes.
  • AI Abilities…: the list window, Abilities, with Turn On or Off (confirmation, Turn On and the ability’s name). WordPress 6.9 or later.
  • Ask the Site…: a question, Question; a confirmation, Run and the ability’s name, before each ability; then a report.
  • Sign Out This Site…: a choice, After signing out: remove the site from QUILL, or keep it in the list.

Tools > WordPress > Your Sites

Across every connected site.

  • Coming Up…: the list window, Scheduled, with Open.
  • What Changed on My Sites…: the list window, Changes, with Open in Browser.
