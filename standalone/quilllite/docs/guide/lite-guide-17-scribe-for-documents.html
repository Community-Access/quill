Chapter 17: Scribe for Documents

Some documents are hard to read with a screen reader. A scanned letter is only a picture of text. A badly made PDF reads its columns in the wrong order, and its tables come out as a jumble of words. QUILL Lite can read a lot of this on your own computer. When it cannot, Scribe for Documents can.

Scribe for Documents is a service from Pneuma Solutions. You send it a document, and it sends back an accessible one: Word, accessible HTML, tagged PDF, EPUB, Kindle, DAISY with audio, MP3 audio or braille. It rebuilds the headings, the reading order and the tables, and it can describe the pictures. QUILL Lite’s Scribe commands are Powered by Scribe for Documents.

Nothing leaves your computer until you say so, every time. Before each upload QUILL Lite shows you what will be sent, and where, and waits for you to press Send and Convert.

QUILL has the same Scribe commands, and a few more. They share one sign-in, one list of conversions and one Options window, so you can use both. This chapter covers QUILL Lite. QUILL’s guide has the same material, plus QUILL’s braille view, watch folder and streaming tab.

Turning Scribe for Documents on

Scribe for Documents is off when you install QUILL Lite, because it sends documents to another company. Nobody should choose that for you. While it is off, it has no menu, no commands and no connection of any kind.

There are two ways to turn it on:

  • Tools > Customize Features (Ctrl+Alt+F10). Check Scribe for Documents (sends the documents you choose to Pneuma Solutions), and press Save. The row’s description says what it does before you check it.
  • When a document needs it. If you import a scanned PDF with File > Import / Convert Document (OCR)…, the question offers Turn on Scribe for Documents and convert. Choose it, and QUILL Lite turns the area on and opens the Convert dialog. When you press Send and Convert, you then hear “Scribe for Documents is on. It is in the File menu.”

Of the profiles, only Everything turns it on. If you reach a Scribe command while the area is off, you hear “Scribe for Documents is off. Turn it on in Tools, Customize Features.”

Where everything is

Menu What is there
File > Scribe for Documents Convert a File…, Convert This Document…, Export with Scribe for Documents…, Convert from a Link…, My Scribe Documents, Conversions, Scribe Status, Repeat Last Scribe Message, Sign In…, Sign Out, Open My Scribe Account in Browser, Options…, About Scribe for Documents

From the keyboard, File > Scribe for Documents is Alt+F, then B. Window > Scribe Conversions is Alt+W, then V.

Every command is also in the Command Palette (Ctrl+Shift+P): type “Scribe”. None has a key of its own. You can give any of them one in Tools > Keyboard Manager (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space).

What it is, and when to use it

Scribe runs on Pneuma Solutions’ computers, not yours. So it can do work that is too heavy for a laptop: it reads scanned pages, rebuilds tables, writes picture descriptions with AI, and records an audio book with a natural voice.

QUILL Lite already reads Word files, PDFs with real text, and scanned pages with free OCR on your computer, with nothing uploaded. Try that first. Use Scribe when:

  • the free OCR gives you a mess, or loses the tables and headings;
  • the reading order or headings of a PDF are wrong;
  • you need a format QUILL Lite cannot make: tagged PDF, Kindle, DAISY with audio, MP3, braille with Scribe’s tables, or large print Word.

Scribe costs pages, and the document goes to the United States. The free way costs nothing and keeps everything on your computer.

Your account and pages

You use your own Scribe account, and pay Scribe for your own pages. QUILL Lite never takes payment, and never sees your Scribe password. These are Pneuma’s prices, which Scribe sets:

What What you get
A new account 100 free pages, to use within 30 days
A page pack From $10 for 200 pages. Packs never run out of date
A subscription $99 a year for 10,000 pages
Human review Scribe’s people fix a document by hand. It costs extra
No pages left Scribe still makes a free preview of the first two pages

Pages are charged once for each document, when it is first converted. Another format of the same document uses no pages.

If your organization pays for Scribe, your pages may come from its shared credits. QUILL Lite says so, for example “1,240 pages left, using credits from Northside College.”

Signing in

  1. Choose File > Scribe for Documents > Sign In…. Any command that needs you signed in starts this for you.
  2. Your browser opens, and you hear “Your browser is open at Scribe for Documents. Sign in there, then come back to QUILL Lite.”
  3. Sign in, or make an account, in the browser.
  4. Come back. The window says, for example, “Signed in to Scribe for Documents as Sam Lee. 100 pages left.” Press Continue.

If the browser did not open, press Copy Sign-in Link and paste the link into any browser. Try Again starts over after “Sign-in timed out. Try again?”. One sign-in covers QUILL Lite and QUILL. Sign Out forgets it for both, and asks first if conversions are waiting.

What you can send and get back

Scribe takes 23 kinds of file, up to 500 MB each: PDF (locked ones too); JPEG, GIF, PNG, TIFF, BMP, DCX, PCX, JPEG 2000 and DjVu pictures; DOC, DOCX, RTF, ODT and TXT; XLS, XLSX and ODS; PPT, PPTX and ODP; and EPUB and MOBI. It does not take web pages. A link must point straight at a file.

Format Good for What QUILL Lite does with it
Accessible HTML Reading with real headings Opens it in a new QUILL Lite window as a readable document, with its headings, lists, tables and links, never as HTML markup
Word Reading and editing. Can be large print Opens it in a new QUILL Lite window
Tagged PDF A PDF screen readers can read. Can be large print Saves it. Open Result and Next Steps open it in a new QUILL Lite window, as text; it never opens by itself
EPUB Ebook readers QUILL Lite does not read EPUB. When QUILL is installed, it opens there. Otherwise it is saved, and the result sentence says once: “QUILL Lite does not open EPUB books. QUILL does.”
Kindle (MOBI) Older Kindle devices Opens it in a new QUILL Lite window, with its pictures, and headings made from its large text
DAISY (text and audio) Talking-book players, and Quill Media Player with chapters Opens it in Quill Media Player
MP3 audio Any audio player Opens it in Quill Media Player
Braille (BRF) Braille displays and embossers Opens it as text in a new QUILL Lite window
Web page for offline reading The accessible HTML with its pictures inside, as one web page (or a zip) Saves it, and says where. Next Steps opens it in your browser

Results go in Documents\Scribe for Documents, in a folder for each document. QUILL Lite never writes over a file: it adds “(2)” instead.

A result opens after its sentence. A new QUILL Lite window, or Quill Media Player, comes to the front only once QUILL Lite has said what it made, between 1.5 and 6 seconds later depending on how long the sentence is. That way your screen reader’s announcement of the new window does not cut the sentence off. If you move to another program while it waits, the result does not open; use Open Result in Scribe Conversions when you are ready.

The Web page for offline reading never opens as text. Every picture is inside it, so in an editor each one would be a single very long line. Next Steps opens it in your browser; so does Open Result, and Open in My Scribe Documents, when it is the only thing there is to open. “Opening the Web page for offline reading in your browser.” Open in My Scribe Documents opens what Open Result would, Word before the Kindle book, not just the file downloaded last.

The Scribe commands

  • Convert a File… opens a file picker that shows only what Scribe takes. You can choose several files.
  • Convert This Document… sends the document you are working on. If it has changes, QUILL Lite saves it first. A Markdown or HTML document goes as Word, or as plain text when Pandoc is not installed.
  • Export with Scribe for Documents… does the same, with Tagged PDF checked the first time. It is how QUILL Lite reaches every Scribe format, although its own export is HTML.
  • Convert from a Link… asks for the address of a file. Scribe fetches it.
  • My Scribe Documents lists your documents on Scribe, and the Trash.
  • Conversions, and Window > Scribe Conversions, open the list of jobs.
  • Scribe Status says what the running job is doing, for example “Notes.pdf, adding image descriptions, 40 percent, about 2 minutes.”
  • Repeat Last Scribe Message says the last Scribe message again.
  • Sign In…, Sign Out and Open My Scribe Account in Browser. Your account page is where you change how long documents are kept, buy pages, and delete your account.
  • Options… and About Scribe for Documents.

When a PDF looks scanned

File > Import / Convert Document (OCR)… asks when a PDF has little or no text. With Scribe on, the choices are Run free OCR on this computer (nothing is uploaded), Convert with Scribe for Documents (rebuilds headings, tables and picture descriptions; uses about 12 pages of your Scribe pages), and Open the text there is. With Scribe off, the middle one reads Turn on Scribe for Documents and convert. The Scribe choice converts to Word, which opens in a new window.

The Convert dialog

Every command opens one dialog, Convert with Scribe for Documents. In Tab order:

  1. What will be sent, a read-only text box with focus. It says which file goes where, how long it is kept, that pictures may be described by AI and that Pneuma does not train AI on documents. Before your first upload it adds the privacy notes. It ends: “Convert only documents you own, have permission for, or that are out of copyright.” Enter here is the same as Send and Convert.
  2. What you want back, nine check boxes. They start as you chose last time.
  3. Settings shown, a line naming the groups of settings below.
  4. Language and voice: Language (or Detect automatically), Dialect, Voice, Play Sample (Escape stops it), Gender of the voice when the language is detected, and Speed, from 0.5 to 4 times.
  5. Braille, when Braille (BRF) is checked: Braille table, Cells per line (20 to 42), Split into pages, and Lines per page (20 to 30).
  6. Print, when Word or Tagged PDF is checked: Large print.
  7. Pictures and maths: Describe the pictures, Mathematics (beta), and Email me when it is finished (Scribe’s own email). Mathematics does not make MathML today: equations come as pictures whose alt text reads the equation in words. The email, one for each format, starts unchecked unless you checked it last time.
  8. Also save as my Scribe account defaults, off every time.
  9. Password for the file, only when a PDF needs one, with Show password.
  10. Pages, for example “This uses about 34 of your 100 pages.”, and Buy Pages.
  11. Send and Convert, or Convert for a document already in your account, and Cancel.

Describe the pictures and AI help. While the AI help area is off, the box starts unchecked and says “(off because AI is turned off in QUILL Lite)”. You may still check it: the descriptions are Scribe’s own work, not QUILL Lite’s AI help.

For a document already in your account, the language, pictures, maths and large print were fixed at upload. They are listed in a read-only line, Fixed when Notes was uploaded, instead.

Scribe Conversions

Window > Scribe Conversions lists every Scribe job on this computer, from QUILL Lite and QUILL. It stays open beside your document, and jobs carry on when you close it or close QUILL Lite.

  • Conversions has three columns: File, Formats and Stage.
  • Details has the percent, time left, Scribe’s latest message, the files made and the folder.
  • The buttons: Open Result, Next Steps…, Open Folder, Cancel Waiting, Retry, Buy Pages, Enter Password…, Sign In…, Ask for Human Review…, Copy Details, Remove from List and Close.

In the list, Enter opens the result, Delete removes the row, and the Applications key lists every command. Escape closes the window.

When What you hear

Next Steps in QUILL Lite

Next Steps… lists what you can do with what was made:

  • Send to Media Player, for MP3 audio or DAISY.
  • Open in QUILL Lite, for Word, Accessible HTML or braille.

QUILL has more here, such as Read Aloud and its braille view. QUILL Lite has no braille view, so a braille file opens as text.

My Scribe Documents

File > Scribe for Documents > My Scribe Documents lists every document in your Scribe account, with Show: Documents / Trash at the top.

  • Download Formats… (Enter) makes or downloads a format. Another format of a document already there uses no pages.
  • Open in QUILL Lite opens a file you already downloaded.
  • Rename…, Move to Trash (Delete), Recover and Delete Forever…. Delete Forever asks first, with Cancel as the default, and cannot be undone. Scribe empties the Trash after 7 days.
  • Ask for Human Review…, Copy Details and Refresh.

Scribe for Documents Options

File > Scribe for Documents > Options… is the one Options window for QUILL Lite and QUILL. A change here is a change in both. It has your account and pages, Sign In or Sign Out, Default formats, the Download folder, Open each result when it is ready (Open it, Ask me first or Don’t open), Offer Scribe when a PDF has little or no text, Speak each stage (Only while a QUILL app is in front, Always or Never), and the About text with buttons for Scribe’s terms, privacy policy and manual. Press OK to save.

Two walkthroughs

A scanned PDF to Word

  1. Choose File > Import / Convert Document (OCR)… and choose the PDF.
  2. QUILL Lite finds little text. Choose Convert with Scribe for Documents, or Turn on Scribe for Documents and convert if it is off, and press OK.
  3. Sign in if you are asked, and press Continue.
  4. Read What will be sent. Check that Word is checked.
  5. Press Send and Convert. You hear “Scribe for Documents: sending Letter.pdf, for Word.”, then each stage.
  6. You hear “Scribe for Documents made Letter.docx in Documents\Scribe for Documents\Letter. Word is open in QUILL Lite.” It is in a new window.

Another format, no pages used

  1. Choose File > Scribe for Documents > My Scribe Documents.
  2. Arrow to the document and press Enter.
  3. The dialog says “No file is sent.” The formats you do not have yet say “, no pages used”. Check MP3 audio, and press Convert.
  4. You hear “Scribe for Documents is making MP3 audio from Notes.”, and then the result. The MP3 opens in Quill Media Player.

QUILL’s guide has four more walkthroughs, including a password-protected PDF: press Send and Convert, type the password in Password for the file, and press it again. If it was wrong, the job waits in Scribe Conversions; use Enter Password… there.

Scribe for Documents in the other QUILL apps

QUILL Lite shares Scribe for Documents with the rest of the QUILL family: one sign-in, one set of Options, one queue and one Scribe Conversions window. Quill Media Player can turn a document into a book to listen to, Quill Converter converts documents as well as audio, QUILL Studio makes a narration with chapters, QUILL Cast and QUILL Social convert a document an episode or a post links, and Quill Beacon keeps places in what Scribe made. A job started in any of them shows in QUILL Lite’s Scribe Conversions too, and only the app running it speaks.

In those apps the Scribe commands have keys of their own. They are not QUILL Lite’s keys: here the same keys do editing work, and the Scribe commands have no key until you give them one. Each app’s user guide has its own Scribe for Documents section.

Privacy, copyright and Safe Mode

  • Where it goes. Pneuma Solutions processes the document in the United States: on AWS in Ohio, with Microsoft Azure (East US) for speech and text recognition, Mathpix for mathematics, and Google Gemini through OpenRouter for picture descriptions. Pneuma never uses documents to train AI.
  • How long it is kept. Under your Scribe retention setting: 30 days unless you changed it, 1 to 30 days by choice, or as long as you like with a subscription. An organization sets 1 to 30 days. Change it with Open My Scribe Account in Browser.
  • Picture descriptions. Pneuma is reviewing its picture-description provider, and makes no promise yet about how long that provider keeps data.
  • Deleting. My Scribe Documents, Move to Trash, then Delete Forever in the Trash.
  • On your computer. A PDF password is used once and never saved. QUILL Lite keeps your sign-in in Windows’ secure store. Your results are saved in your own folders and never shared with anyone else who uses this computer.
  • Copyright. Convert only documents you own, have permission for, or that are out of copyright.
  • Safe Mode turns every Scribe command off. You hear “Scribe for Documents is disabled in Safe Mode. Restart QUILL Lite without –safe-mode to use network features.”

When something goes wrong

You hear one sentence that names Scribe for Documents and the file. A code, such as QUILL-SCRIBE-PAGES, is in Copy Details, never spoken.

Code What you hear, in short What to do
QUILL-SCRIBE-SIGNIN “Your Scribe for Documents sign-in has ended.” Sign In… on the job in Scribe Conversions
QUILL-SCRIBE-SIGNIN-LOCAL No port could be opened on this computer, or your browser did not open Allow QUILL Lite in your security software, or use Copy Sign-in Link
QUILL-SCRIBE-PAGES Scribe’s message, then “Use Buy Pages in Scribe Conversions, then Retry.” Buy Pages, then Retry
QUILL-SCRIBE-PASSWORD “Notes.pdf needs its password. Open Scribe Conversions to enter it.” Enter Password…; three tries, then Retry
QUILL-SCRIBE-BUSY “Scribe for Documents asked QUILL Lite to slow down; trying again in a minute.” Nothing: it tries again
QUILL-SCRIBE-NOTDOC Scribe does not take this kind of file, or web pages Save it as Word or PDF first
QUILL-SCRIBE-TOOBIG The file is over 500 MB Split it, or make it smaller
QUILL-SCRIBE-GONE “No longer in your Scribe account” Remove from List, and convert the file again
QUILL-SCRIBE-OFFLINE “Reconnecting to Scribe for Documents; your conversion is still running there.” Nothing: it carries on when the connection is back
QUILL-SCRIBE-SAVE The folder is not writable or could not be created Choose another Download folder in Options, then Retry
QUILL-SCRIBE-OFF Scribe is disabled in Safe Mode Restart QUILL Lite normally
QUILL-SCRIBE-FAILED Scribe’s reason, or “Something went wrong. Please try again.” Retry; then Copy Details for support

QUILL’s guide explains each one in full.
