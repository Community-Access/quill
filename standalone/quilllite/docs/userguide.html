QUILL Lite User Guide

For QUILL Lite 2.0.0, the beta, November 2026. The last published version is 1.1.2. Help > About (Alt+F1) shows the exact build you have.

This is the guide to QUILL Lite, the text editor for people who use a screen reader. You can open any text file, change it, and save it back exactly the way it came in, and every chapter here is about doing one part of that well.

Each chapter is a page of its own. This page is the contents: every chapter, two sentences on what it covers, and links to its main topics, so you can go straight to the part you need.

How to use this guide

You do not have to read any of this before you start. Open QUILL Lite, type, and press Ctrl+S. When you want more, Chapter 1: Your first minutes is the place to begin, and after that you can read in order or jump to the chapter you need.

  • On this page, each chapter is a level 3 heading. Press 3 in JAWS or NVDA to move from chapter to chapter, and Tab or the down arrow to hear its topics.
  • Every chapter page starts with the chapter’s title as a level 1 heading. Its main topics are level 2 headings, and their parts are level 3 and below.
  • At the bottom of every chapter are links to the chapter before it, the one after it, and back to these contents.
  • The QUILL Lite User Guide shortcut in the Start menu opens this page. Inside the app, F1 answers in every window, and Ctrl+F1 lists every key.
  • If you would rather read the whole guide as one book, userguide.epub, in the docs folder beside QUILL Lite, has every chapter in it, in order.
